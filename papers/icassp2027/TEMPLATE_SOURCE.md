# ICASSP 2027 template provenance

Downloaded 2026-09-05 from the official ICASSP 2027 Paper Kit:

- [Paper Kit](https://cmsworkshops.com/ICASSP2027/papers/paper_kit.php)
- [Complete template archive](https://cmsworkshops.com/ICASSP2027/papers/PaperFormat/ICASSP2027_Paper_Templates.zip)

`official-template/` retains the new archive and its original template sources.
No manuscript text or style files were imported from the previous ICASSP draft.
The source archive's opening comment still says ICASSP-2026, but its title and
contents specify ICASSP 2027 and it is the archive linked by the 2027 Paper Kit.

The unmodified sample compiled with `/opt/homebrew/bin/tectonic --keep-logs
Template.tex`. Its example URL produced a 2.6863pt overfull box; the new paper
must be checked separately. Do not modify `spconf.sty` or `IEEEbib.bst`.

The official regular-paper deadline is 16 September 2026. The kit permits up
to four pages of technical content and an optional fifth page containing only
references, funding acknowledgements and the applicable ethics statement.
The kit specifies non-blind review, named authors, an approximately 100--150
word abstract and fonts of at least 9pt. This note is a formatting record,
not confirmation that the research is submission-ready.
