\subsection{Data and evaluation scope}
We use DMSTrack's released per-vehicle detections
\cite{chiu2024dmstrack} on V2V4Real~\cite{xu2023v2v4real}.
Nine test-backed validation sequences form the development subset
(1,993 paired frames); 25 train sequences form the larger comparison
(5,601 frames). Both groups and earlier train probes informed fusion
design, and the detector was trained on train. These are previously
used data.

An expanded evaluation covers 43 deduplicated V2V4Real segments: 9,699 paired
frames from 17 recordings. It includes the earlier 9/25 cohorts and three
subsequently acquired validation segments. Only one of those three,
with 409 frames, comes from a previously absent recording. The checkpoint
configuration assigns train/test to training/validation; its complete
history is unverified.

We also evaluate all five paired-vehicle segments in the official
64-beam V2X-Real validation release~\cite{xiang2024v2x}: 619 paired
frames from three collection dates. A single-vehicle segment is excluded
by input structure before scoring. Detector, calibration, tracker and
communication settings remain fixed. V2X scores were first inspected
after development selection; these inputs are now exposed for later work.
The companion evidence records all selection and exposure events.

\subsection{Shared local tracking and score likelihood}
We track planar car centers within 40 m of either sensor and the
annotated ego-frame rectangle
$[-70.4,70.4]\times[-40,40]$ m, excluding a 3 m disk around each vehicle.
Truth and estimates use the same domain; each local sensor uses its own
40 m support. Detections retain the released confidence cutoff of 0.20.

All methods share the score-aware LBP-LMB local update. The primary
constant-velocity model uses current ego coordinates, a 0.1 s interval,
survival probability 0.99, acceleration intensity 25 m$^2$/s$^3$,
position covariance $I_2$ m$^2$, and in-domain $p_D=0.9$.
Poisson clutter has mean three per sensor scan and density $3/(\pi40^2)$.
Each previous-scan detection creates an independent local birth with
existence 0.01, zero velocity, and covariance
$\operatorname{diag}(16,16,225,225)$. We prune $r\leq0.001$, moment-project
retained mixtures to one Gaussian, and extract MAP cardinality.
Geometric methods share history qualification and absent-label
censoring, with in-support censor existence 0.001.

Following post-hoc probability calibration~\cite{niculescu2005calibration},
we fit $u(s)=\sigma(a\,\operatorname{logit}s+b)$, $a\geq0$, by
sequence-balanced binary log loss with regularization $0.001a^2$.
Offline per-sensor one-to-one assignment within 12 m supplies labels.
Development sequences use leave-one-sequence-out fits; the 25-sequence
corpus and all new data use the full-nine fit, $a=2.8858$, $b=2.1564$.
The score enters each local association likelihood once through
\begin{equation}
 L(s)=\frac{u(s)}{1-u(s)}\,\frac{1-\pi}{\pi},
 \label{eq:mark}
\end{equation}
where $\pi$ is the sequence-balanced fitting prevalence.

\subsection{Communication and baselines}
Reliable links deliver both directed packets each scan. Intermittent
links combine independent directed loss of 0.1 with a bidirectional
outage over the middle 20\% of scans. Methods share the delivery trace
and one packet opportunity per direction per scan. Receivers pool local
and delivered neighbor updates with weights $[0.5,0.5]$; otherwise they
keep the local update. Accounting uses 16,384-byte fragments and a
256-byte control charge per scan. Delivery is independent of packet
length. GCE uses $\rho=0.25$ and $\lambda=5$ s.

\emph{No-age KLA} uses qualified weights $\alpha,b$.
\emph{Recency} replaces $b$ with $q$ in existence, and
\emph{Conservative} uses $\beta$ without a current correction.
\emph{Capped-score}/\emph{Capped-cal.} bound recency existence by
$\min(r_{\rm rec},\max(r_{\rm noage},U))$, where $U$ is the largest
association-weighted raw/calibrated score among currently observed,
upweighted sources with existence at least 0.5.

\emph{Scalar} retains the original spatial pool $p_0$ and integral $I_0$
while applying GCE's scalar gates without a curvature test.
\emph{Guarded Scalar} (GS) additionally uses the identical source
curvature rejection and aggregate fallback as GCE on its own inputs,
but returns $p_0$ and
$\operatorname{logit}r=\sum_j\beta_jz_j+
\sum_j\bar\kappa_j\delta_j+\log I_0$.
GS runs its own full recursion. \emph{Fixed Ratio} retains GCE's joint
update, guard conditions, and history weights, replacing both sign-dependent supports
by $\eta$. The lowest two-condition mean OSPA on the nine development
sequences selects $\eta=0$ from
$\{0,0.05,0.1,0.125,0.25,0.5,1\}$, then holds it fixed.
Zero strength retains the history-weighted base pool.
GS and Fixed Ratio require full Gaussian ratio packets.

\emph{MIL-AM} adapts the public manuscript's augmented assignment and
common/exclusive subspace fusion~\cite{gao2019milpreprint}.
Symmetric KL cost with unmatched cost 50 per side aligns labels;
geometric methods share this alignment. Common labels use arithmetic
existence pooling and existence-weighted spatial mixtures; exclusive
labels retain their source density. \emph{TC-5}/\emph{TC-10} use the
author's kinematic matching and two-stage multi-neighbor functions
\cite{nguyen2021tc}, with five-/ten-scan histories, order one, and cutoff
12 m. They retain separate local filters. \emph{Local} omits communication.

\subsection{Metrics and recursive controls}
We use $p=2,c=12$ m for position OSPA~\cite{schuhmacher2008ospa}
and missed/false squared GOSPA costs~\cite{gospa2017} ($\alpha=2$).
Common-target RMSE pools robot--time--truth matches shared by both
methods. Sequences receive equal weight. Descriptive 95\% intervals
use 10,000 paired whole-sequence percentile resamples of the old 9/25
cohorts, without multiplicity adjustment. For the expanded V2V corpus,
we first average paired differences within each recording, then resample
the 17 recording means. V2X results are descriptive because only three
collection dates are represented.

Scalar, GS, joint correction without the guard, and GCE form a complete
recursive correction--curvature $2\times2$ comparison. We report GCE--GS
and the interaction $(\mathrm{GCE}-\mathrm{GS})-
(\mathrm{joint\ without\ guard}-\mathrm{Scalar})$.
Further ablations remove the history switch or positive score constraint,
retaining the shared local score model. A separate fixed-input diagnostic
substitutes base/corrected means and integrals at identical GCE-visited
inputs; alternate outputs never feed back into its recursion.

\subsection{Motion, detection probability, and correlation}
Raw absolute poses match every old sequence and reproduce released
relative sensor transforms. After CV prediction and birth creation,
known planar yaw/translation maps positions, velocities, and full
covariances to the current ego frame, equivalently implementing planar
world-frame CV. No-age, Scalar, GS, and GCE run on all 34 old and three
new segments. Separately, modeled $p_D\in\{0.7,0.8,0.9,0.95\}$ varies on
the nine development sequences. Both checks retain the same measurements,
domains, calibration, and fusion settings, without primary reselection.

To distinguish integrability from statistical reliability, we also
simulate a two-dimensional, conditional-existing Gaussian state with
prior covariance $25I_2$ and two measurements of noise covariance $I_2$.
Their cross-covariance is $\varrho I_2$, for
$\varrho\in\{0,0.25,0.5,0.75,0.9\}$, with 10,000 samples per setting.
We compare equal posterior pooling, unit-admission GCE assuming
independent errors, and the oracle with known joint covariance, reporting
RMSE, normalized squared error, and nominal 95\% region coverage.
This control does not estimate the real tracker's covariance coverage.
