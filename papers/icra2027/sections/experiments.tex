\section{Experiments}
\subsection{Real detections, sequence scope, and local model}
We use the released per-vehicle, no-fusion detections from DMSTrack
\cite{chiu2024dmstrack} on V2V4Real~\cite{xu2023v2v4real}.
The development subset contains all nine released validation sequences
(1,993 paired frames). The main evaluation uses 25 train sequences
(5,601 paired frames), excluding the seven train IDs previously used in
initial transfer checks. Each sequence is complete. The released detector
was trained on the train split. All 34 reported sequences informed method
development, so this is a fusion development evaluation and not an
independent detector or generalization test.

Inputs include each vehicle's detections, detection scores, per-frame
relative transforms, and car annotations. Released detections already use
a confidence cutoff of 0.20; we apply no further score threshold.
We evaluate planar car centers within 40 m of either sensor and inside
the annotated ego-frame rectangle
$[-70.4,70.4]\times[-40,40]$ m, excluding a 3 m disk around each vehicle.
The same domain is applied to estimates and truth, and each local sensor
uses its own 40 m range. This range models sensing support rather than
occlusion. Coordinates follow the current ego frame; the constant-velocity
model does not compensate ego motion.

Every method in the main comparison uses the same score-aware LBP-LMB
local update. The model uses a 0.1 s scan interval, survival probability
0.99, white-acceleration intensity 25 m$^2$/s$^3$, position observation
covariance $I_2$ m$^2$, in-domain $p_D=0.9$, and nominal Poisson clutter
mean three per sensor scan. The clutter density is $3/(\pi40^2)$.
Each sensor independently creates a birth at every previous-scan
detection with existence 0.01, zero velocity, and covariance
$\operatorname{diag}(16,16,225,225)$. Bernoullis with $r\leq0.001$ are
pruned, retained mixtures are moment-projected to one Gaussian, and
MAP-cardinality extraction produces the reported set.

Observation-history qualification is identical across the geometric
density controls. In the single-Gaussian implementation, an absent label
is eligible for an existence censor when its represented mean is inside
the absent source's modeled FoV and expected detection is at least 0.1.
The censor's existence bound is the transmission threshold 0.001.
Missing spatial densities do not participate in the spatial product.

\subsection{Common detection-score model}
Calibration labels are obtained by one-to-one, per-sensor geometric
assignment of detections to truth within 12 m. We fit
$u(s)=\sigma(a\,\operatorname{logit}s+b)$ with $a\geq0$ by minimizing
sequence-balanced binary log loss plus $0.001a^2$.
Each development sequence uses a fit on the other eight.
For the 25-sequence evaluation, the fit uses all nine development
sequences and remains fixed: $a=2.8858$, $b=2.1564$.
Ground truth is used for this offline fit and evaluation; online births,
association, and fusion use detections and communicated beliefs.

The local detection-association likelihood is multiplied once by
\begin{equation}
 L(s)=\frac{u(s)}{1-u(s)}\,\frac{1-\pi}{\pi},
 \label{eq:mark}
\end{equation}
where $\pi$ is the sequence-balanced positive fraction of the fitting
data. Probabilities are clipped to $[10^{-6},1-10^{-6}]$ before this
conversion. Treating the resulting score ratio as a separable mark
likelihood is an approximation. The main baselines receive exactly the
same ratios, so their comparison with GCE does not attribute local
calibration gains to the fusion correction.

\subsection{Communication and comparison methods}
Reliable links deliver both directed packets at every scan.
Intermittent links use independent directed loss of 0.1 and a common
bidirectional outage over the middle 20\% of scans. Every method shares
the same delivery realization and one packet opportunity per direction
per scan. A receiver with a delivered neighbor uses weights $[0.5,0.5]$;
otherwise it retains its own local update. Geometry and poses are supplied
inputs. Byte totals include a modeled 256-byte control charge per scan
and 16,384-byte packet fragments. The loss trace does not depend on
packet size.

\emph{No-age KLA} uses the qualified spatial and existence weights
$\alpha,b$. \emph{Recency} replaces $b$ with $q$ in existence only.
\emph{Conservative} uses $\beta$ from~\eqref{eq:baseweights} without a
current correction. The internal \emph{Capped-score} and
\emph{Capped-cal.} controls bound recency existence by
$\min(r_{\rm rec},\max(r_{\rm noage},U))$, where $U$ is the largest
current association-weighted raw or calibrated score among upweighted
sources with input existence at least 0.5. These controls preserve the
ordinary spatial pool. \emph{Scalar} retains GCE's base and positive and
negative gates but corrects only existence log-odds, leaving the old
spatial density and normalizer.

\emph{MIL-AM} implements the public author manuscript's augmented label
assignment and common/exclusive label-subspace fusion
\cite{gao2019milpreprint}. Gaussian labels are assigned by symmetric
KL cost with unmatched cost 50 per side; the same alignment is used by
the geometric density methods. Common labels use arithmetic existence
pooling and existence-weighted spatial mixtures, and exclusive labels
retain their source density. This is an adaptation of the public
manuscript, not a verified reproduction of the complete later TAES
experiment~\cite{gao2022differentfov}.

\emph{TC-5/TC-10} call the author's kinematic track matching and
two-stage multi-neighbor fusion functions~\cite{nguyen2021tc}, with
five- or ten-scan histories, matching order one, and cutoff 12 m.
They use independently labeled tracks from the same local backend.
As in that architecture, the fused track output does not feed back into
local densities. We evaluate set estimates without network-wide label
reconciliation. \emph{Local} uses the shared backend without communication.

\subsection{Metrics and component checks}
The primary metric is position OSPA with $p=2$ and cutoff 12 m
\cite{schuhmacher2008ospa}. We also report GOSPA with
$p=2,c=12$ m,$\alpha=2$~\cite{gospa2017}; its missed and false components
are \emph{squared costs} in m$^2$.
For localization, each method is assigned to truth separately, then errors
are pooled on robot--time--truth instances matched by both methods.
Common support and RMSE are reported together.

Sequences receive equal weight. Paired 95\% percentile intervals use
10,000 resamples of complete sequences, with no multiplicity adjustment.
Frames and vehicles are not independent replicates, and the intervals
are descriptive because the trajectories have informed development.
Every declared sequence and both link conditions are retained.

All three GCE ablations run complete recursive trajectories:
no curvature guard, no conservative history switch, and no positive
score constraint. Single-Bernoulli checks cover the centralized
shared-prior identity, conflicting observations, zero gates, single-source
behavior, and nonintegrable aggregates. A separate Python implementation
reconstructs local Gaussian ratios and the fused existence, mean, and
covariance, and recomputes trajectory metrics and packet counts.
Finally, the zero-vector codec is replayed on all 34 primary trajectories
under both link conditions, requiring identical tracking outputs and
original local and fusion diagnostics.
