Cooperative posteriors mix inherited and current information, which age
weighting cannot control separately. We propose guarded current-evidence
fusion (GCE), admitting bounded Bernoulli posterior-to-prediction ratios
using sensing opportunity, score and miss support, and Gaussian curvature.
Admitted ratios jointly update spatial density and existence through a
shared normalizer. Across 43 V2V4Real segments with a common local
tracker, GCE reduces mean OSPA by
\FollowGainNoAgeReliable\%/\FollowGainNoAgeIntermittent\% over qualified
geometric pooling under reliable/intermittent links. Recursive controls
show a smaller gain over equally guarded scalar correction; the
recording-level interval includes zero under intermittent links.
Transfer to five V2X-Real segments does not improve on pooling.
Exact zero omission reduces fragmented traffic by
\CodecWireSavingReliable\%/\CodecWireSavingIntermittent\% relative to
full GCE packets on the original comparison corpus, with remaining
overhead over pooling. These results support selective current-evidence
admission on the evaluated V2V corpus while exposing limits in transfer
and in gains beyond scalar correction.
