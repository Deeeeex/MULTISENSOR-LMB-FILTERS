Cooperative multi-object tracking must distinguish new observations from
information inherited through earlier communication. Posterior age
weighting acts on both components together, limiting control over current
sensing evidence. We propose guarded current-evidence fusion (GCE), which
augments conservative geometric pooling with bounded Bernoulli
posterior-to-prediction ratios. An admission rule combines current sensing
opportunity, association-weighted detection-score and miss support, and
Gaussian curvature to select local information increments. A closed-form
Gaussian implementation jointly updates spatial density and target
existence through a shared normalizer, allowing admitted evidence to
influence both localization and cardinality. Across 43 V2V4Real segments
evaluated with a common local tracker, GCE reduces mean OSPA by
\FollowGainNoAgeReliable\% and \FollowGainNoAgeIntermittent\% relative to
qualified geometric pooling under reliable and intermittent links,
respectively. An exact zero-omission code preserves GCE trajectories while
reducing fragmented traffic by \CodecWireSavingReliable\% and
\CodecWireSavingIntermittent\% relative to full GCE packets on the original
25-segment comparison corpus.
