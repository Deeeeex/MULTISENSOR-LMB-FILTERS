Cooperative trackers exchange posteriors that mix inherited information
with current observations. Weighting these posteriors by age cannot
control the two contributions separately. We propose guarded
current-evidence fusion (GCE), which augments a conservative posterior pool
with bounded Bernoulli posterior-to-prediction ratios. Each admitted ratio
jointly updates the spatial density and existence probability through a
shared normalizer. A Gaussian curvature guard makes this correction
tractable under approximate association, and an exact zero-vector codec
reduces its communication cost. On 25 V2V4Real sequences with a common
score-aware local tracker, GCE reduces OSPA by \GainNoAgeReliable\% and
\GainNoAgeIntermittent\% relative to qualified geometric pooling under
reliable and intermittent links. It also improves on recency weighting
and scalar-only correction in both conditions. The gain combines lower
missed-target and false-target costs with better common-target
localization. Exact zero omission cuts fragmented communication by
\CodecWireSavingReliable\% and \CodecWireSavingIntermittent\% relative to
full Gaussian packets while preserving the tracking outputs. These
results demonstrate the benefit of restoring current evidence jointly
across the two parts of a Bernoulli belief.
