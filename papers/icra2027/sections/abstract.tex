Cooperative posteriors mix inherited and current information, which age
weighting cannot control separately. We propose guarded current-evidence
fusion (GCE), admitting bounded Bernoulli posterior-to-prediction ratios
using sensing opportunity, score and miss support, and Gaussian curvature.
Admitted ratios jointly update spatial density and existence through a
shared normalizer. On 25 previously used V2V4Real sequences with a common
local tracker, GCE reduces OSPA by
\GainNoAgeReliable\%/\GainNoAgeIntermittent\% over qualified geometric
pooling under reliable/intermittent links. Recursive controls show a
smaller advantage over equally guarded scalar correction; the
correction--guard interaction intervals include zero.
GCE has lower mean OSPA on three newly acquired segments, but
underperforms pooling on the sole previously absent recording.
Correlated Gaussian observations also yield undercovered spatial regions
despite positive semidefinite increments. Exact zero omission reduces
fragmented traffic by
\CodecWireSavingReliable\%/\CodecWireSavingIntermittent\% relative to
full GCE packets, with remaining overhead over pooling.
These results support selective admission on the evaluated corpus while
limiting claims about generalization and uncertainty.
