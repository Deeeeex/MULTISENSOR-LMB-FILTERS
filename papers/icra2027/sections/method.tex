\section{Guarded Current-Evidence Fusion}
\subsection{Local prediction, update, and information support}
Each robot predicts its LMB, updates it with the current local measurement
set, and exchanges one posterior packet with available neighbors. Fusion
output becomes the next local prior. A label is aligned across sources
before fusion; births remain local and measurement driven.
For a represented label, write the local prediction and update as
$f_j^-=(r_j^-,p_j^-)$ and $f_j^+=(r_j^+,p_j^+)$.
The Bernoulli density has empty-set mass $1-r$ and singleton density $rp(x)$.
Our replay moment-projects $p_j^-$ and $p_j^+$ to four-dimensional
Gaussians over planar position and velocity.

A direct sensing opportunity occurs when the modeled detection
probability at a predicted track is positive. Both a detection and a
missed detection advance the local direct-opportunity timestamp $\tau_j$;
receiving another robot's packet does not advance it. A separate history
flag records whether a represented belief descends from an informative
local update. When informed inputs exist, untouched priors are excluded.
An absent label contributes an existence censor only if its source could
observe the represented target. Otherwise that source abstains. Censors
carry no spatial density. This support handling is shared with the
qualified no-age and recency controls.

Let $\mathcal P$ be eligible represented sources and $\mathcal E$ eligible
existence sources, including qualified censors. Normalize ordinary
delivered weights $w_j$ separately on the two supports:
\begin{equation}
 \alpha_j=\frac{w_j\mathbf1[j\in\mathcal P]}{\sum_{k\in\mathcal P}w_k},
 \qquad
 b_j=\frac{w_j\mathbf1[j\in\mathcal E]}{\sum_{k\in\mathcal E}w_k}.
 \label{eq:ordinary}
\end{equation}
All sums below use the corresponding support. A label without spatial
support is omitted. For a represented source, define
\begin{equation}
 a_j=\rho+(1-\rho)e^{-(t-\tau_j)\Delta t/\lambda},
 \qquad q_j=\frac{b_j a_j}{\sum_k b_k a_k},
 \label{eq:age}
\end{equation}
with $a_j=\rho$ before any local opportunity and $a_j=1$ for a censor.
We use $\rho=0.25$ and $\lambda=5$ s.

\subsection{A conservative inherited base}
Set $z_j=\operatorname{logit}(r_j^+)$ and choose the existence weights
\begin{equation}
 \beta=
 \begin{cases}
 q,&\sum_j(q_j-b_j)z_j<0,\\
 b,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:baseweights}
\end{equation}
Thus history can lower the base existence log-odds but cannot raise them.
The spatial base retains the ordinary weights $\alpha$.
An unnormalized base has masses
\begin{align}
 A_0&=\prod_j(1-r_j^+)^{\beta_j},\\
 A_1(x)&=\prod_j(r_j^+)^{\beta_j}
                \prod_jp_j^+(x)^{\alpha_j}.
 \label{eq:base}
\end{align}
These are separate existence and spatial weights; when the weights and
supports coincide, the base reduces to ordinary Bernoulli KLA.
The no-history ablation sets $\beta=b$ throughout. Its purpose is to test
the small conservative history adjustment separately from the current
evidence correction.

\subsection{Admitting current positive and negative evidence}
The local existence increment is
\begin{equation}
 \delta_j=\operatorname{logit}(r_j^+)
          -\operatorname{logit}(r_j^-).
 \label{eq:increment}
\end{equation}
The local tracker incorporates a detection score $s$ through a fitted
positive-to-negative mark ratio $L(s)>0$. Let $W_{jm}$ be the normalized
local association mass for assigning this Bernoulli to measurement $m$,
and let $A_j=\sum_m W_{jm}$. For a current sensing opportunity, use
\begin{align}
 g_j^+&=\sum_m W_{jm}
       \max\!\left(0,\frac{L(s_m)-1}{L(s_m)+1}\right),
       \label{eq:positivegate}\\
 g_j^-&=(1-A_j)\frac{p_{D,j}}{2-p_{D,j}}.
       \label{eq:negativegate}
\end{align}
Both gates are zero without a current opportunity. The first admits
positive increments in proportion to association-weighted mark
discrimination; the second weights the missed-detection branch by its
bounded likelihood contrast. They satisfy $g_j^+\leq A_j$,
$g_j^-\leq1-A_j$, and hence $g_j^++g_j^-\leq1$.
They are bounded admission scores, not calibrated posterior error bounds.

If at least two eligible sources represent the label and none of the
eligible existence inputs is a censor, define
\begin{equation}
 \kappa_j=(1-\beta_j)
 \begin{cases}g_j^+,&\delta_j\geq0,\\g_j^-,&\delta_j<0.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:kappa}
\end{equation}
Otherwise set every $\kappa_j=0$. This restriction avoids constructing an
unavailable current ratio for an absent label. The no-score ablation
replaces the contrast in~\eqref{eq:positivegate} by one, retaining the
same score-aware local tracker and setting $g_j^+=A_j$.

\subsection{Joint existence and spatial normalization}
For each admitted source, multiply the same fractional Bernoulli ratio
$(f_j^+/f_j^-)^{\kappa_j}$ into both hypotheses in~\eqref{eq:base}.
The resulting spatial product is
\begin{equation}
 h(x)=\prod_jp_j^+(x)^{\alpha_j}
       \prod_j\left[\frac{p_j^+(x)}{p_j^-(x)}\right]^{\kappa_j},
 \qquad I=\int h(x)\,dx.
 \label{eq:product}
\end{equation}

\noindent\textbf{Proposition 1 (normalized Bernoulli form).}
For $0<r_j^-,r_j^+<1$ and $0<I<\infty$, normalizing
these empty and singleton masses yields
\begin{align}
 p^*(x)&=h(x)/I,\label{eq:spatial}\\
 \operatorname{logit}(r^*)&=\sum_j\beta_j z_j+
                    \sum_j\kappa_j\delta_j+\log I.
                    \label{eq:existence}
\end{align}
\emph{Proof.} The empty mass is
$H_0=A_0\prod_j[(1-r_j^+)/(1-r_j^-)]^{\kappa_j}$.
The integrated singleton mass is
$H_1=I\prod_j(r_j^+)^{\beta_j}
       \prod_j(r_j^+/r_j^-)^{\kappa_j}$.
Taking $\log(H_1/H_0)$ gives~\eqref{eq:existence}; normalizing the
singleton shape gives~\eqref{eq:spatial}.\hfill$\square$

A scalar-only correction keeps the first two terms
in~\eqref{eq:existence} but uses the old spatial pool and its integral.
It therefore implements a different approximation from the full admitted
Bernoulli ratios. We evaluate this scalar rule as a complete recursive
component control, in addition to substitutions on fixed fusion inputs.

In the special case of a common prior $f^-$, equal weights
$\alpha=\beta$, unit gates, and independent exact local observations,
$\kappa_j=1-\beta_j$. The full product then reduces to
$\prod_j f_j^+/(f^-)^{M-1}$, the standard shared-prior Bayesian
combination~\cite{wu2022sharedpriors}. This algebraic check does not
make approximate LMB associations or correlated detector errors exact.

\subsection{Gaussian correction and curvature guard}
For $p=\mathcal N(m,P)$, write
\begin{equation}
 \log p(x)=-\tfrac12x^\top Jx+v^\top x+c,
 \quad J=P^{-1},\quad v=Jm,
\end{equation}
where $c=-[d\log(2\pi)+\log|P|+m^\top Jm]/2$ and $d=4$.
A source transmits the differences
$(\Delta J_j,\Delta v_j,\Delta c_j)$ between its update and prediction.
The symmetric precision difference needs ten numbers, the information
vector four, and the constant one.

Moment projection can widen a covariance after ambiguous association,
so this Gaussian ratio need not be log-concave. GCE rejects the entire
source correction, including its scalar increment, when
\begin{equation}
 \lambda_{\min}(\Delta J_j)<
 -10^{-10}\max(1,\|J_j^+\|_2,\|J_j^-\|_2).
 \label{eq:curvature}
\end{equation}
Let $\bar\kappa_j$ be the resulting weight. No eigenvalue is clipped.
The aggregate natural parameters are
\begin{align}
 J&=\sum_j\alpha_jJ_j^++\sum_j\bar\kappa_j\Delta J_j,\\
 v&=\sum_j\alpha_jv_j^++\sum_j\bar\kappa_j\Delta v_j,\\
 c&=\sum_j\alpha_jc_j^++\sum_j\bar\kappa_j\Delta c_j.
 \label{eq:natural}
\end{align}
For positive definite $J$, the normalized quantities are
\begin{align}
 P^*&=J^{-1},\qquad m^*=J^{-1}v,\\
 \log I&=c+\tfrac12[d\log(2\pi)-\log|J|+v^\top J^{-1}v].
 \label{eq:integral}
\end{align}
Use $\bar\kappa$ in~\eqref{eq:existence} as well. A nonfinite aggregate,
failed Cholesky factorization, or reciprocal condition estimate below
$10^{-12}$ discards all residual terms and restores the base. When no
residual is admitted, the original base implementation is retained exactly.
In exact arithmetic, positive semidefinite admitted precision gains and
a positive definite base ensure a positive definite aggregate; the final
test also covers numerical failures. The no-curvature ablation removes
only~\eqref{eq:curvature}, retaining this necessary integrability test.

Existence log-odds use a $[10^{-9},1-10^{-9}]$ numerical clip, and
differences within $10^{-12}$ do not activate the history switch.
Current gates and ratios are cleared after fusion; the next packet
recomputes them from that robot's next local update.
For state dimension $d$ and $M$ eligible sources, the dense Gaussian
arithmetic costs $O(Md^3)$ and adds $O(d^2)$ numbers per track.

\subsection{Exact zero-vector transmission}
The full Gaussian implementation extends a 232-byte scalar Bernoulli
summary by fifteen 64-bit numbers, giving 352 bytes per track plus a
32-byte packet header. Many local updates leave all fifteen differences
exactly zero. A two-byte tag distinguishes a full vector from an omitted
zero vector and preserves the sign bit of each omitted zero. All nonzero
vectors retain their original 120 bytes. For $n$ tracks and $k$ nonzero
vectors, the actual packet length is
\begin{equation}
 B=32+234n+120k\quad\text{bytes}.
 \label{eq:packet}
\end{equation}
This codec has no threshold or quantization parameter. Decoded numerical
values, and hence the fusion rule, are unchanged. We verify complete
recursive trajectories with the codec rather than estimating its savings
from the mean number of tracks alone.
