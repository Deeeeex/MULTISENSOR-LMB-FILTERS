\subsection{Development results and the frozen decision}
The initial Age-all arm passed a coarse direction screen against ordinary
and FoV-aware KLA, but lost to the stronger Lineage control and worsened
localization on common targets. Existence-only recency then reduced
development OSPA against Lineage by 5.6\% and 14.0\% in the two event
families, while retaining seven false estimates in 2,880 no-new
robot--frames; FoV had none. The confirmation gate eliminated those extra
false estimates but increased churn OSPA by 60.5\% against Lineage.
Neither the v2 nor v3 combination met its full balanced screen. We retained
these failures, froze the rules, and ran the new-seed study to assess the
conditional mechanism rather than searching for a passing threshold.

Table~\ref{tab:main} includes all 540 validation runs.
Against Lineage, ER changes mean OSPA from \LineageSplit\ to \ErSplit\ m
in split--rejoin and from \LineageChurn\ to \ErChurn\ m in
churn--departure. The paired differences are
\DeltaSplit\ m [\DeltaLowSplit, \DeltaHighSplit] and
\DeltaChurn\ m [\DeltaLowChurn, \DeltaHighChurn], respectively.
In the no-new control, the difference is
\DeltaNonew\ m [\DeltaLowNonew, \DeltaHighNonew].
Brackets denote the descriptive bootstrap intervals defined above.
Cardinality error decreases in all three families. In the predeclared
reunion windows, OSPA is 2.533 versus 3.264 m in split--rejoin and 0.926
versus 1.248 m in churn--departure (ER versus Lineage).

FoV and the two MIL variants have higher OSPA in these inputs. Support
partitioning improves MIL in the event families but does not remove its
localization and false-target tradeoffs. These results apply to the stated
shared-label specialization and finite mixture cap, and do not establish
superiority over complete published multi-view or label-matching systems.
The large Local error reflects the deliberately global evaluation during
separation; it should not be interpreted as a failed local detector.

\subsection{Where the age factor belongs}
Table~\ref{tab:common} and Fig.~\ref{fig:outcomes}(b) distinguish existence
gains from localization. With the same lineage qualification, L+Age-all
increases common-target RMSE relative to Lineage by 16.8\% in split--rejoin
and 3.1\% in churn--departure. ER's ratios are 0.9999 and 0.9998, over
57,988 and 58,936 common triples. Those differences are negligible relative
to measurement noise. This supports the fixed-input motivation in
Fig.~\ref{fig:mechanism}: recent direct sensor opportunity is useful for
existence weighting, but does not measure the total spatial information
retained in a posterior. The recursive result is empirical and specific
to these scenes; the proposition alone does not establish it.

\subsection{False hypotheses and the confirmation tradeoff}
The false-cost results materially limit the conclusion
(Table~\ref{tab:false}). In development ER lowered post-departure false
cost by 34.5\% against Lineage. In validation it instead increases the mean
from 10.785 to 12.255 m$^2$. The paired difference is $+1.470$ m$^2$
[$-0.705$, $3.795$]; the development improvement does not reproduce.
Similarly, ER's no-new false cost is 0.570 m$^2$, versus 0.00375 m$^2$
for FoV. Lower OSPA therefore does not imply better false-track rejection.

Confirmed ER reduces no-new false cost to 0.00375 m$^2$, but its churn
OSPA is 1.519 m, versus ER's 0.846 m. This is consistent with the cost of
requiring consecutive local positive associations during short sensing
opportunities. In churn, ER acquires all 160 opposite-subteam robot--target
queries across twenty episodes; Confirmed ER acquires 152. Among acquired
queries, mean delays are 2.47 and 7.63 s, respectively; the eight censored
confirmed queries are excluded from the latter mean and remain failures.
The gate is a disclosed ablation, not an accepted remedy.
The unsmoothed trajectories in Fig.~\ref{fig:time} show why discovery and
post-departure persistence should be evaluated separately.

\subsection{Accounting and verification}
All communicating arms have exactly the same attempted and delivered
messages for each paired episode. Mean attempted wire cost is 24.98 MiB
for split/no-new and 25.74 MiB for churn. Across communicating arms and
families, mean unpadded payload totals range from 2.14 to 9.85 MiB; all
occupy the same padded slots. The metric
audit recomputes 518,400 validation robot--frames and verifies 1,169 frozen
source hashes. Focused checks verify signed age behavior, the
no-opportunity fallback, source-order invariance, fixed-input spatial
invariance, and exact v2 replay under the added confirmation metadata.
These checks establish the stated implementation properties and agreement
between the two metric implementations.
