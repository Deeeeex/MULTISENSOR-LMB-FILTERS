\subsection{Lower set error across both link conditions}
GCE achieves the lowest OSPA among all main comparison methods
(Table~\ref{tab:main}). It improves on No-age KLA by
\GainNoAgeReliable\%/\GainNoAgeIntermittent\% and on Recency by
\GainRecencyReliable\%/\GainRecencyIntermittent\%
(reliable/intermittent). The paired 95\% OSPA intervals lie below zero against every geometric reference,
including both capped variants and Scalar. The improvement is thus
consistent across several ways of weighting inherited posteriors.
Figure~\ref{fig:paired} complements these aggregate results by pairing
each sequence's gains across the two link conditions, separating
consistent gains from condition-specific reversals.

The gain improves several aspects of tracking together. Relative to
No-age KLA, missed-target cost falls by
\MissGainNoAgeReliable\%/\MissGainNoAgeIntermittent\% and false-target
cost by \FalseGainNoAgeReliable\%/\FalseGainNoAgeIntermittent\%.
Common-target RMSE also drops from \LocNoAgeReliable\ to
\LocMainNoAgeReliable\ m under reliable links and from
\LocNoAgeIntermittent\ to \LocMainNoAgeIntermittent\ m under intermittent
links. Recency exhibits the same direction of improvement across these
metrics. GCE therefore improves target recovery and localization while
suppressing false tracks. Its OSPA also remains below the arithmetic
MIL-AM and both track-consensus adaptations.

\subsection{Joint correction and component ablations}
Table~\ref{tab:ablation} separates the Scalar reference, three component
removals, and the complete GCE rule. GCE improves on Scalar in both
conditions, with both paired OSPA intervals below zero.
It reduces missed-target cost and common-target
localization error, with a small increase in false-target cost.


The curvature guard lowers mean OSPA in both conditions, with a paired
interval below zero under intermittent links. It also suppresses the
large position shifts produced by unsuitable Gaussian ratios; removing
it activates the aggregate fallback twice. The conservative history
switch makes a smaller contribution. Removing the positive score
constraint reduces missed-target cost but substantially increases
false-target cost, showing its role in controlling positive evidence.
For the history and score removals, paired total-OSPA intervals include
zero. Together, the results support the complete joint correction over Scalar
and show how the positive score constraint controls false tracks.

The nine-sequence development subset shows the same mean OSPA ordering
of GCE, Scalar, and No-age KLA. Detection-score modeling also improves
the local backend: unmarked No-age KLA is weaker than its score-aware
counterpart. Because every main method shares the latter, the main
comparison measures the additional improvement from fusion.

\subsection{What changes at a fixed fusion input?}
The corrected mean and integral give the lowest OSPA on identical GCE
inputs (Table~\ref{tab:fixedinput}). Updating the mean at fixed corrected
existence reduces OSPA by about 0.003 m, with both paired intervals
below zero. The joint change from base/base is
\JointReliableBothMean/\JointIntermittentBothMean\ m. Its intervals and
those of changing the integral at corrected means include zero. These
substitutions isolate immediate effects. Recursive Scalar also changes
curvature admission and future inputs through prediction, association,
and track extraction.

\subsection{Behavior around a communication outage}
GCE has lower mean OSPA than both references in every phase
(Fig.~\ref{fig:phases}). Under intermittent links, its differences from
No-age KLA are \PhaseIntermittentBeforeNoAgeMean,
\PhaseIntermittentOutageNoAgeMean, and \PhaseIntermittentAfterNoAgeMean\ m
before, during, and after the outage. The before/after intervals lie
below zero; the outage interval includes zero. Without packets, receivers
continue local tracking, so remaining gains reflect previously fused
beliefs. When exchange resumes, the mean advantage returns to its
pre-outage magnitude.

\subsection{Communication--accuracy tradeoff}
The zero-vector codec preserves the full Gaussian GCE trajectories while
reducing raw payload by
\CodecRawSavingReliable\%/\CodecRawSavingIntermittent\%.
Figure~\ref{fig:communication} shows the resulting shift toward lower
communication at unchanged OSPA. With fragmentation and control traffic,
the reduction is
\CodecWireSavingReliable\%/\CodecWireSavingIntermittent\%
(Table~\ref{tab:communication}). Encoded GCE requires
\CodecWireExtraNoAgeReliable\%/\CodecWireExtraNoAgeIntermittent\% more
fragmented bytes than No-age KLA, in exchange for its lower tracking error.
The codec thus recovers much of the cost of joint Gaussian evidence
without weakening the fusion rule.
