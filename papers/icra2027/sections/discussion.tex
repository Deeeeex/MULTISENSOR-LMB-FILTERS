\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
GCE combines selective current-evidence admission with joint spatial
and existence updates for cooperative vehicle tracking.
Across 43 V2V4Real segments, it reduces mean OSPA by
\FollowGainNoAgeReliable\% and \FollowGainNoAgeIntermittent\% relative to
No-age KLA under reliable links and links with packet loss and temporary
outages, respectively. On the 25-segment corpus, exact zero omission
saves \CodecWireSavingReliable\%/\CodecWireSavingIntermittent\% of
fragmented traffic relative to full GCE packets without altering
tracking outputs. Future work will address transfer across datasets
and uncertainty calibration.
