\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
GCE selectively admits approximate current Gaussian Bernoulli ratios
on a conservative pool. Recursive controls support a modest
corpus-level gain beyond equally guarded scalar correction and a larger
gain over the tested constant strength. They do not isolate a
normalizer-only benefit.

The sole previously absent recording exposes a generalization failure
despite better averages over all new segments. Additional independent
recordings and detector splits are needed. Motion and $p_D$ checks
address model dependence but do not supply occlusion-dependent
likelihoods; the Gaussian control shows that a PSD guard cannot certify
covariance consistency. Zero omission recovers part of the additional
communication cost. Larger teams and measured radio links remain
necessary before broader deployment claims.
