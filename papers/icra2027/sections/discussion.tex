\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
GCE restores bounded current evidence on an inherited posterior pool
and normalizes the resulting Bernoulli jointly. A spatial update changes
both localization and the singleton mass that determines existence.
Closed-form Gaussian fusion and source admission make this coupling
practical for approximate local tracking. The complete rule improves
on the main references under both link conditions; fixed-input and
outage analyses explain its immediate effects and temporal behavior.
Exact zero omission reduces communication at identical tracking output.

The present evaluation uses approximate associations, a fitted score
model, planar two-vehicle tracking, and emulated links. A curvature test
ensures admissible Gaussian correction but does not resolve unknown
cross-source correlation. Future evaluation will use new routes,
detector splits, larger teams, and measured radio links.
