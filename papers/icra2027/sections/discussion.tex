\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
GCE restores bounded current evidence on top of an inherited posterior
pool and normalizes the resulting Bernoulli jointly. This coupling is
central: a spatial update changes both target localization and the
singleton mass that determines existence. The Gaussian form supplies
closed-form fusion, while source admission controls approximate local
increments. Across both communication conditions, the complete rule
improves on the main fusion references and combines lower missed and
false costs with better localization relative to no-age and recency
pooling. Exact zero omission makes the additional evidence cheaper to
communicate at identical tracking output.

The present evaluation uses approximate associations, a fitted score
model, planar two-vehicle tracking, and emulated links. A curvature test
ensures admissible Gaussian correction but does not resolve unknown
cross-source correlation. Evaluation on new routes and detector splits,
larger robot teams, and measured radio links will test transfer beyond
the current corpus. The main finding is that current evidence is most
useful when its spatial and existence effects are retained together.
