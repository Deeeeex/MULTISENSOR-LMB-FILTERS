\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
GCE selectively admits approximate current Gaussian Bernoulli ratios.
Recursive controls support a modest gain beyond guarded scalar correction,
with an intermittent recording-level interval crossing zero; they do not
isolate a normalizer-only benefit.

The absent V2V recording and V2X-Real expose transfer failures.
Motion and $p_D$ checks do not supply occlusion-dependent likelihoods,
and a PSD guard cannot certify covariance consistency.
Zero omission recovers part of the communication overhead. Independent
recordings, larger teams and measured radio links remain necessary.
