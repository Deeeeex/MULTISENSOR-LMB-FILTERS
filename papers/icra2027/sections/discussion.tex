\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
GCE separates a conservative inherited pool from admitted current local
updates, then normalizes their Bernoulli product jointly. The Gaussian
form makes explicit why a spatial ratio also changes existence: the
integral of the corrected spatial product is part of the singleton mass.
The complete 25-sequence result improves on qualified no-age pooling,
recency weighting, conservative and capped controls, and the Scalar
component in both link conditions. Relative to the two original geometric
controls, missed cost, false cost, and common-target localization improve
together.

The ablations narrow the interpretation. The full spatial correction has
a supported advantage over Scalar in the main corpus. The curvature
guard prevents two invalid aggregates observed in its removal, but its
OSPA effect is not resolved in both conditions. The history switch has
a small, uncertain contribution. The score constraint exchanges fewer
false targets for more misses, so its merit cannot be inferred from
false cost alone. These distinctions favor studying complete Bernoulli
evidence over attributing the entire result to observation age.

Three limits remain. First, current ratios come from approximate
association and Gaussian moment projection; a positive curvature check
does not establish source independence or remove all inherited
correlation. Second, the fitted mark model depends on geometric
calibration labels and a separability approximation. All real sequences
have informed development, and the main detections come from the
detector's training split. A new route and detector split is needed to
test generalization. Third, the replay is planar, involves two vehicles,
uses uncompensated moving ego coordinates, and emulates communication.
Larger robot networks, pose uncertainty, and measured radio effects
remain outside this experiment.

Within that scope, the result establishes a concrete fusion improvement:
bounded current evidence can be restored while keeping existence and
spatial normalization consistent. Exact zero omission recovers part of
the extra communication cost without changing a track or a probability.
