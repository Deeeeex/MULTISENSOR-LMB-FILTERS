# V66 influence-aware decision breadth

## Why V65 is insufficient

V65 measures the network-additive amount of receiver-supported existence
mass rescued by withholding a cross-formation posterior.  The fresh convoy
t=80 result shows that this is an amplitude measure, not a network-impact
measure: `1.030%` risk produces only `+0.315%` mean tracking gain because two
threshold-changing label decisions are concentrated at one receiver and do
not create broad three-step improvement.

## Current-only impact model

Let `W` be the registered receiver-row/sender-column KLA fusion matrix.  For
each receiver-label pair whose candidate existence crosses the extraction
threshold upward, V66 computes a robust crossing margin

`m = association_support * min(threshold - r_reference,
r_candidate - threshold)`.

The column sum of `W^h` is the first-order amount of network influence carried
by a perturbation at that receiver after `h` fusion rounds.  V66 sums the
margin-weighted column influence for `h=0,1,2`, then divides by the number of
sensor-time cells.  The resulting dimensionless robust decision exposure
distinguishes three quantities that V65 conflates:

1. how much existence mass is rescued;
2. how many receiver-label extraction decisions change robustly; and
3. how widely those changes can propagate through the planned fusion graph.

V66 retains every V65 event, coverage, useful-information, downward-crossing,
payload, and connectivity guard.  It adds a development gate requiring robust
decision exposure of at least `0.05`, numerically tied to the existing 5%
strong-gain target.  This is a calibrated current-only proxy, not a theorem
that guarantees 5% E-OSPA improvement.

## Evidence protocol

The first attribution uses five already-opened development states: four
strong radial M24/X36 cases and the weak convoy t=80 counterexample.  The
formula and `0.05` threshold must be committed before any new relay,
merge-split, or curved-corridor tracking outcome is opened.  A fresh scene is
the next falsification test; the opened attribution cannot support a held-out
or validation claim.
