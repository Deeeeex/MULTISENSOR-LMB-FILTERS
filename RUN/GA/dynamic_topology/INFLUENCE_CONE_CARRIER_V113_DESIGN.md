# V113: carrier-direction influence-cone screen

## Decision question

V112 closes duration-only recovery: every three-to-six-step action remains
below five percent and locally unsafe.  V110 shows that F1 is neutral before
the protected F2 posterior reaches it and then becomes the only negative
formation.  V113 asks whether the carrier direction, rather than another
release page, can move this delayed protected-to-safe boundary to a less
vulnerable part of the network.

## Frozen arms

The screen starts from the same X36 seed-211 t=72 posterior and H=8 random
streams.  It reuses the registered counter-clockwise full-payload outcome and
executes two new arms:

| Arm | Residual carrier | Payload action |
|:--|:--|:--|
| primary reference | counter-clockwise | full |
| alternative static | clockwise | full |
| interaction candidate | clockwise | V110 F2--F5 abstention schedule |

The existing V110 counter-clockwise abstention result remains separate opened
mechanism evidence.  Every new carrier is a strong sensor-level residual
cycle, keeps the same dominant and residual fusion weights per receiver,
keeps the same directed message count, uses only physical edges and must pass
rolling B3.

## Strong comparison

The interaction candidate is compared with the better of the two full-payload
directions.  It passes only with at least five percent mean and mature-page
gain, nonnegative worst-sensor, formation and F6-peer terminal gains,
nonnegative consensus and communication changes, and rolling B3.  A gain over
only the counter-clockwise reference is insufficient if clockwise full
payload is already better.

The V110 formation set is outcome-selected, so a pass establishes only that
carrier-conditioned abstention has action-space headroom.  It would authorize
the observable influence-cone predictor and safety projection, not a
deployment or generalization claim.

## Opened result

| Arm | Mean E-OSPA | Gain vs CCW | Gain vs mean-best full | B3 |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|:--:|
| counter-clockwise full | 84.037151 | -- | -- | pass |

| clockwise F2--F5 abstention | 78.479689 | +6.613% | +4.063% | pass |

The interaction does not pass the strong gate.  Its mature-page minimum is
`+2.588%`; formation gains against clockwise full are approximately
`[0.000, 0.979, 10.620, 4.576, 9.913, -1.495]%`; and the F6 non-gateway
terminal gain is `-7.206%`.  Consensus improves by `+11.699% / +17.191%`
and attempted bytes fall by `3.330%` relative to clockwise full.

The carrier interaction is real, but binary cycle orientation moves the
delayed protected-to-safe boundary from F1 to F6 instead of removing it.
V113 therefore closes whole-cycle clockwise/counter-clockwise switching as a
policy output.  The next action-space screen must expose receiver-selective
shielding or label-complete gradual entry at the affected boundary and must
retain both static directions in the baseline set.
