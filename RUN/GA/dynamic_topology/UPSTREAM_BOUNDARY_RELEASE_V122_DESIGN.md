# V122: release the upstream F5 transformation

V113 abstains from the incoming cross-formation residual at F2--F5.  F6 is
never directly protected, yet it becomes the only negative formation after
the altered F5 posterior reaches it.  V114--V121 change F6's input, sender,
receiver, timing and carrier order, but none repairs the tail.

V122 tests the remaining upstream causal explanation.  It keeps the clockwise
carrier fixed and retains explicit abstention in F2--F4, while F5 consumes its
ordinary full cross-formation posterior on every page.  If F5 no longer
creates the altered state passed to F6, F6 should recover without adding a new
path or changing the fusion weights.

Only this one candidate is run.  The action must reach at least 5% mean gain
over clockwise full, keep at least 5% gain on every mature page, make every
formation and the F6 peer tail nonnegative, and retain nonnegative worst-node,
consensus, byte and rolling-B3 results.  Failure closes formation-specific
upstream release rather than triggering a subset sweep.

V122 is chosen from opened V113--V121 evidence and is development-only.
