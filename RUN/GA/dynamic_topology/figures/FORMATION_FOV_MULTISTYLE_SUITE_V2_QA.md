# Formation-FoV Multistyle Suite v2: Figure Contract and QA

## Five-point figure contract

1. **Core conclusion.** Under the same `120° / 300 m` directional-sensing
   hardware, offset-corridor convoy and linear relay create two distinct,
   geometry-qualified information-flow regimes; orthogonal crossing remains a
   deliberately difficult stress case.
2. **Evidence source.** The figure uses only trajectories, formation
   membership, shared boresights and the initial-geometry reference skeleton
   generated by the frozen v5 scene code at development seed `41`, time `80`.
   It reads no posterior, learned score, routing output or tracking metric.
3. **Archetype.** This is a schematic-led, asymmetric composite.  Convoy and
   crossing retain equal metre-scale axes in square panels, while the long
   relay corridor receives a full-width panel instead of being compressed
   into an equal-width grid cell.
4. **Visual channels.** Deep-blue circles show sensors, red diamonds show
   targets, blue curves show formation-centre motion, pale-red curves show
   exact target-group-centre routes, grey dashed lines show the `t=1`
   geometry-derived formation skeleton, and teal sectors show the exact FoV
   of one deterministic representative sensor per formation.  All
   representative sectors are outlined; only one per panel is lightly filled.
5. **Deliverables.** Matplotlib is the exclusive renderer.  SVG and PDF are
   editable vector masters; the `2160 x 1440` PNG is the 300-dpi document
   preview.

## Source binding and evidence boundary

The source contract is
`formation-fov-multistyle-figure-source-v2`.  Every scene carries
`sceneGeometryVersion=formation-fov-multistyle-v5`, its registered scene
SHA-256, geometry-gate state and `trackingOutcomeAuthorized=false`.  The
renderer rejects a non-v5 source, altered sensing hardware, missing digest,
within-formation heading drift, an incorrect scene state, or any source that
claims to consume a posterior or tracking result.

The JSON retains all six M24/X36 scenes so the cross-scale contract remains
machine-checkable.  The displayed schematic uses X36 because its six
formations expose the complete large-scale layout.  M24 follows the same
local scene-generation and hardware rules with four formations; duplicating a
second row made the wide relay corridor unreadably small.  The experiment
table and caption state both scale definitions explicitly.

The `80/80` absolute and `40/40` paired held-out v5 results establish geometry
eligibility for convoy and relay.  They are not plotted and do not authorize a
routing or tracking claim.  Crossing remains `stress-only-v5`.

## English caption

**Figure X | Non-radial formation layouts under identical directional-sensor
hardware.** The X36 configuration is shown at seed 41 and time 80; M24 uses
the same local geometry and sensing rules with four rather than six
formations. Deep-blue circles and red diamonds denote sensor and target
positions, while blue and pale-red curves denote formation-centre and
target-group-centre trajectories. To avoid visual occlusion, each formation
shows the exact `120° / 300 m` field of view of one deterministic
representative sensor; short blue arrows indicate the boresight shared by all
six sensors in that formation, and the sectors are not formation-level
coverage unions. Grey dashed lines are the formation-level projection of the
reference skeleton built from the initial geometry, not dynamic routes chosen
at the displayed time. Offset-corridor convoy and linear relay passed the
preregistered v5 geometry-eligibility gate, whereas orthogonal crossing
remains a stress-only scene. The figure is generated from frozen scene
geometry and uses no posterior or tracking result.
