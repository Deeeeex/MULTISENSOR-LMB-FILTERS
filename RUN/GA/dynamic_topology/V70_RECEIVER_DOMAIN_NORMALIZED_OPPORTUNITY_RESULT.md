## Action direction

Alternative transport is the common cross-scale mechanism.  M24 nominates
transport at all five actionable times and no quarantine action.  X36
nominates transport at all sixteen actionable times; quarantine is additionally
nominated only at t=40 and t=52.  At the strongest X36 anchor, transport is
preferred because formation 5 has 3.891% local alternative net utility versus
1.675% local quarantine net utility, while formation 4 contributes another
1.349% local alternative utility.

This points to a fixed-message route replacement, not further communication
sparsification, as the next executable method.  The candidate sender swaps
must still be bundled by affected formation and projected through current
physicality, message-count parity, mutual receiver-slot consistency, and
rolling three-step sensor/formation connectivity.
