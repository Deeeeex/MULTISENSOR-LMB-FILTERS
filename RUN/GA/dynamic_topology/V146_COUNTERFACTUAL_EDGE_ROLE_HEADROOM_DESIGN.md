# V146 pre-outcome freeze: counterfactual edge-role headroom bank

V145 closes global cadence and formation-load threshold tuning on the M24
development state.  V146 asks the narrower pre-learning question: does a
finite, scale-equivariant bank of one-payload edge-role vectors contain a
gate-safe action on both M24 and X36?  A negative answer closes learned W/R
role scheduling; a positive answer authorizes on-policy teacher generation,
not a communication-method claim.

## Fixed carrier and temporal projector

The physical route, attempted opportunities, delivery uniforms, nominal KLA
weights and V134 protection action remain fixed.  Every attempted edge sends
exactly one current mixture-aware LMB posterior.  Cross-formation edges always
send the reference lineage R.  A selected protected intra-formation receiver
may receive the working lineage W on continuation offsets `1, 2, 3, 5`; all
other edge-times send R.

The resulting envelope is `R-W-W-W-R-W-R-...`.  It contains at most three
consecutive W pages, restores every selected edge to R before the late pulse,
adds no auxiliary payload and retains all-R as the feasible incumbent.  A W
role is suppressed automatically when its receiver is no longer protected.

## Closed spatial action bank

Formation ranks come from the already frozen V134 action order, not numeric
formation or sensor IDs.  The same seven patterns are resolved against four
M24 ranks and six X36 ranks:

| Index | Pattern | M24 ranks | X36 ranks |
|--:|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | first rank | `{1}` | `{1}` |
| 2 | last rank | `{4}` | `{6}` |
| 3 | odd ranks | `{1,3}` | `{1,3,5}` |
| 4 | even ranks | `{2,4}` | `{2,4,6}` |
| 5 | upper half | `{1,2}` | `{1,2,3}` |
| 6 | lower half | `{3,4}` | `{4,5,6}` |
| 7 | all ranks | `{1,2,3,4}` | `{1,2,3,4,5,6}` |

Each pattern induces a complete edge-level R/W vector: only intra-formation
edges whose receiver belongs to a selected, currently protected formation can
carry W.  The bank is frozen at `2026-08-29 01:43 CST`, before any V146
tracking outcome is opened.  No singleton, pair, temporal offset or threshold
may be added after seeing a V146 result.

## Sequential gate

M24 actions are screened against the existing intervention, full-window,
mature-window, minimum-sensor, minimum-formation, exact-rejoin, zero-auxiliary
and no-byte-increase gates.  Only M24-passing action indices may open the
matching X36 screen.  At least one identical pattern must pass independently
on both scales to establish pre-learning headroom.

All screens remain repository-only.  Joint headroom permits an on-policy
counterfactual teacher and deterministic rolling projector; it does not by
itself permit a deployable-method, validation or paper-result claim.  If no
M24 action passes, X36 is not opened.  If M24 passes but every matching X36
action fails, the role-scheduling route is closed before GNN training.
