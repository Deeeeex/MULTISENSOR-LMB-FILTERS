## Method consequence

Stop output-cardinality and threshold sweeps.  A deployable successor must
predict or preserve a rare label--state correction before it is lost, using
observable association support, mixture overlap, innovation and edge
history.  The learned model may rank candidate edge--label roles, while a
deterministic projector preserves the effective KLA graph and one-payload
budget.  Exact relay state is a teacher only and may not appear as an
uncharged runtime input.

The main progress document may report this one joint mechanism conclusion
with its `+89%` payload caveat.  Per-cell tables, failed V140/V141 variants
and the single-scale checkpoints remain repository-only.
