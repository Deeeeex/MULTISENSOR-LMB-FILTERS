# V122 finding: F5 carries the delayed debt into F6

## Result

V122 keeps the fixed clockwise carrier and V113 explicit abstention in F2--F4,
but restores full cross-formation participation in upstream F5 for all eight
pages.  It preserves the 60-message graph, weights, physicality and rolling
B3.  The arm does not pass the tracking gate.

| 79.585550 | +2.711% | -1.409% | +0.640% | -0.014% | -0.000% | +0.000% | +1.710% |

The causal result is sharp despite the failed aggregate gate.  Releasing F5
repairs the V113 F6-peer terminal loss by `+6.722%` and makes F6 numerically
equal to clockwise full.  Thus the delayed F6 regression is primarily carried
by the altered F5 posterior; it is not an intrinsic defect of the F5-to-F6
gateway geometry.  However, releasing F5 also removes nearly all of F5's
large local benefit, reducing network gain from `+4.063%` to `+2.711%`.

## Decision

A single upstream release cannot meet the network objective, and no F2--F5
subset sweep is authorized.  The useful remaining headroom is heterogeneous:
different formations prefer different carrier rows and participation modes.
An optimistic whole-window formation oracle over CW full, V113, V121 and V122
reaches `+5.293%`; a page-wise oracle reaches `+6.275%` and exceeds 5% from
the fourth page onward.  These stitched outcomes are not executable evidence,
but they justify one bounded row-composition screen.

The next action should let each receiver formation choose a carrier row and
participation mode, then project the joint choice onto exact message, physical
and rolling-B3 constraints.  Only a passing executable projection authorizes
learning formation-row values.

V122 is privileged opened-development evidence at X36 seed 211, t=72, H=8.
It is not deployable, validation or generalization evidence.
