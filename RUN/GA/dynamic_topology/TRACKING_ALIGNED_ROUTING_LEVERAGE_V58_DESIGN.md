# V58: event-conditioned routing leverage before finer actions

## Research decision

V56--V57 close the current formation-level source/trust/protection expansion on
the three uniformly chosen M24 convoy anchors.  Complete action enumeration,
longer holds, and heterogeneous formation combinations all remain below 1.5%
mean tracking gain.  The next experiment must not add another selector to an
action family whose local headroom is already absent.

The earlier V35--V37 results are not direct counter-evidence because they use
different states and a radial formation-FoV scene.  Their strongest M24 states
have formation retention debt above 9%, whereas every current convoy anchor
has maximum debt below the registered 2% intervention threshold.  V58 first
tests whether this observable state difference explains when topology control
has causal leverage.

## Stage A: same-code leverage attribution

Reconstruct fixed-reference posterior states at two already opened cases:

| Role | Preset / seed | Times | Known tracking evidence |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Earlier high/medium gain | `m24-formation-fov / 211` | 60, 72, 104, 124 | V35/V36 paired H=3 outcomes |
| Current low gain | `m24-formation-fov-convoy / 1201` | 60, 100, 132 | V57 complete protection subsets |

The reference cache runs under a new source-only execution context.  It may
produce current posteriors and past topology pages, but it receives no target
truth and is not authorized to score tracking.  This avoids comparing old
stored features with posteriors produced by a different current receiver.

For every state, record two families of observable features:

- the former posterior-contrast and selected-link-stress event score;
- exact formation retention-debt fractions, threshold excess, selected
  formation count, message saving, label-retention floor and decision
  threshold crossings under the registered one-round projection.

The offline paired tracking gains are joined only after these features are
fixed.  The purpose is mechanism attribution, not a validation claim.

## Falsification rule

The old scalar posterior-contrast score is rejected as the primary sentinel
if it remains high on the low-gain convoy states or fails to rank the strong
radial states above them.  Retention debt becomes the primary event variable
only if a single scale-normalized rule separates the opened strong and weak
states without using preset, seed, time, formation identity, truth, or future
measurements.

No event rule is accepted merely because it explains seed 211.  After the
initial attribution, it must select fresh event times from the full opened
convoy reference trajectory and expose meaningful H=3 oracle headroom there.
Low-leverage states should return the reference route; they are not required
to manufacture a positive intervention gain.

## Stage B: action decision

If event-conditioned convoy states recover at least 5% mean H=3 headroom with
the existing safe protection/recovery family, V58 will retain that family and
learn only the event/action value with reference fallback.

If high-debt convoy states still lack headroom, the intervention granularity
moves from one mode per formation to receiver--sender--label decisions.  The
repository already contains the V54 label-selective runtime, exact shared-edge
byte projection, observation-opportunity metadata, and post-receipt retention
projection.  V58 would reuse that execution machinery but replace its failed
posterior-preservation objective with paired H=3 tracking value.  This is not
a revival of the V54 payload-equivalence story: the label action is justified
only by tracking-aligned oracle headroom.

## Scale and scene gate

The method remains open development until it satisfies the original target:

- at least 5% aggregate tracking gain on event-conditioned M24 development
  states, with nonnegative formation, worst-sensor, consensus and byte
  constraints;
- a frozen rule with stable positive gain on X36 rather than an M24-only
  threshold retune;
- later full-episode paired evaluation on radial, convoy, relay, merge-split
  and curved-corridor primary scenes, with crossing retained as stress;
- reference fallback on low-leverage states and no increase in aggregate
  attempted communication.

V58 Stage A does not authorize model training, X36 outcomes, reserved seeds,
or paper-level generalization claims.

## Stage-A result

The same-version radial/convoy scan is complete.  Maximum formation retention
debt has Pearson correlation `0.9495` with the seven best previously opened
M24 H=3 gains, compared with `0.9164` for the former event score.  The frozen
truth-free rule selects fresh convoy times `[40, 88, 128]`; their maximum debt
is `2.937%`, `3.615%`, and `3.941%`, whereas the old uniformly chosen times
60, 100, and 132 remain below `1.55%`.  V59 will open only these three states
for complete safe formation-protection headroom before deciding whether the
action must move to receiver--sender--label granularity.

## Stage-B result

V59 enumerated all 16 formation protection subsets at each selected convoy
state.  The best strict mean E-OSPA gains were only `+0.054%`, `+0.384%` and
`+0.364%`, for a mean of `+0.267%` and `0/3` strong states.  The registered
formation-level headroom gate failed.  Retention debt is therefore not a
sufficient tracking-leverage sentinel, and the absence of gain cannot be
attributed to an incomplete formation-subset search.

The method search now moves to receiver--sender--label actions.  Before
opening another tracking outcome, the next attribution separates locally
measurement-supported labels from unsupported retained labels using the
current posterior's `detectionAssociationMass`.  The full V59 interpretation
and claim boundary are recorded in
`V59_EVENT_CONDITIONED_FORMATION_HEADROOM_RESULT.md`.
