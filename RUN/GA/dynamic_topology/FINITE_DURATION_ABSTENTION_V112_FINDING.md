## Causal conclusion

The experiment reveals a genuine conflict rather than a missing dwell-time
threshold.  Early recovery limits the accumulated network benefit.  Later
recovery increases the benefit and consensus improvement, but the altered
posterior has already entered downstream recursive states.  Returning to the
full source changes future inputs; it cannot reconstruct the counterfactual
state that would have existed without the earlier intervention.

This agrees with V106--V107's switch-shock observation and extends it with a
complete monotone duration family.  Further time-page enumeration is closed.
V112 labels may train or calibrate finite-horizon regret, but duration cannot
be the sole policy output.

## Method decision

The next action must control the time-expanded influence cone.  The first
bounded screen keeps V110's opened safe-formation abstention set and changes
the residual carrier direction:

1. counter-clockwise full payload is the registered primary baseline;
2. clockwise full payload is an alternative-static baseline;
3. V110 counter-clockwise abstention is the existing mechanism arm; and
4. clockwise abstention tests whether redirecting the protected-to-safe
   boundary repairs F1 without losing the aggregate gain.

The interaction arm must beat the better full-payload direction by at least
five percent and retain nonnegative formation, F6-peer, consensus,
communication and rolling-B3 results.  Passing establishes route-conditioned
action-space headroom, not a deployable selector.  Failure closes binary cycle
orientation and moves the action space to receiver-selective shielding or
label-complete gradual re-entry.

V112 is opened development evidence only.  It does not support validation,
generalization or model-training claims beyond use as paired regret labels.
