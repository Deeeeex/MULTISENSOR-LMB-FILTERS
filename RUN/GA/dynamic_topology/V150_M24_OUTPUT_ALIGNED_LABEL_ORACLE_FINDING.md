# V150 M24 output-aligned label oracle finding

## Decision

V150 establishes that explicit source--label KLA participation has real,
stable M24 headroom, but a one-step bounded action does not meet the registered
5% aggregate gate.  V150 therefore stops at M24 and does not open X36.  The
next justified experiment is the narrower V151 repeated-action mechanism
probe; no GNN is authorized.

The broad top-8 action improves tracking and consensus but increases the full

output-inert byte donors also fails to recover the budget.  Singleton byte
effects are therefore not additive.

## Mechanism finding

An omission changes the fused posterior and can alter the number and structure
of labels/components transmitted later.  Immediate payload saving is not a
valid communication objective by itself: the policy must model or constrain
the resulting full-horizon posterior-complexity debt.

The singleton screen also shows that current one-round fused-output impact is
useful for removing completely inert candidates, but it is not a value
function.  Some high-impact candidates have only a small recursive benefit,
one candidate harms the mean score, and the best legal action appears late in
the impact ranking.  A future observable selector therefore needs signed task
value and communication-debt prediction rather than a larger unsigned impact
score.

## Next gate

V151 repeats only the best legal V150 singleton over the full H=8 window while
charging an omission envelope at every step.  It is a privileged mechanism
upper bound, selected from opened M24 outcomes.  If temporal repetition does
not approach the material-gain gate without byte growth, the repeated-action
space is stopped before implementing an adaptive online policy or GNN.
