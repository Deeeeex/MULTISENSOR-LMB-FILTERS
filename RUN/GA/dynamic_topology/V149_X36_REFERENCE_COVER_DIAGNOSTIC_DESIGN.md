# V149 X36 reference-cover mechanism diagnostic

## Why this diagnostic is identifiable

This diagnostic is frozen before any V149 X36 outcome is opened.  It does not
revise the failed registered V149 M24 gate and cannot produce a joint-scale or
deployment claim.

M24 is not an informative falsifier for the specific reference-cover
mechanism.  In V142, all requested M24 spatial augmentations were overwritten
and the final candidate was exactly identical to its predecessor.  X36 was
different: six severe output cells improved only when a missing label was
reintroduced with its reference-supported spatial density.  V149 is the first
single-payload arm that makes every same-edge R label mandatory on W pages.
Its X36 run can therefore answer a narrow causal question that its M24 screen
could not answer: does reference-label cover preserve the rare X36 spatial
state without a second message?

## Frozen execution

- Protocol: unchanged `receiver-relative-label-role-teacher-v149-v1`.
- Preset / seed: `x36-formation-fov` / `1601`.
- Anchor, action, continuation length, physical route, fusion weights,
  delivery uniforms, output readout and paired static reference: resolved by
  the existing V149 protocol with no retuning.
- Every attempted edge carries one complete mixture-aware LMB payload, all R
  labels remain covered, the same-edge full-R bytes are the cap, and auxiliary
  communication is zero.
- Receiver-state access remains privileged and uncharged; the outcome cannot
  support an online communication-method claim.

The run starts only after the already-running V145 X36 transfer audit exits,
so the two CPU-bound screens do not interfere.

## Diagnostic decisions

The mechanism is supported only if all of the following hold:

1. X36 intervention-window E-OSPA gain is at least `+5%`;
2. full and mature gains are nonnegative;
3. minimum sensor and formation gains are no lower than `-0.01%`;
4. registered reentry is exact, auxiliary bytes are zero and attempted bytes
   do not increase;
5. the previously identified severe output cells are repaired without a new
   set of material negative cells.

A positive result authorizes designing an output-aligned and explicitly
charged receiver synopsis, followed by fresh paired M24/X36 validation.  It
does not erase the failed M24 intervention gate.  A failure closes
reference-cover-preserving label-role multiplexing before any row-joint
optimizer or GNN is built.
