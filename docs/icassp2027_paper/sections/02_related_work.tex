\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\textbf{RFS tracking.}
Random finite-set filters provide a Bayesian framework for tracking uncertain
target cardinality and measurement association. PHD filters introduced a
tractable first-moment recursion \cite{Mahler2003PHD}; labeled RFS models add
persistent identities \cite{Vo2014LRFS}; and LMB filters provide a practical
independent-Bernoulli approximation \cite{Reuter2014LMB}. Multi-sensor
GLMB/LMB variants extend this framework to cooperative sensing
\cite{Vo2019MSGLMB}. We do not modify the LMB recursion; we study how much of
the posterior must be communicated before distributed fusion.

\textbf{Conservative fusion.}
When cross-correlations are unknown, conservative rules avoid overconfident
fusion. Covariance intersection handles Gaussian estimates
\cite{JulierUhlmann1997CI}, while KLA/GCI extends the log-opinion-pool idea to
probability densities \cite{Battistelli2014KLA}.
Related log-opinion-pool analyses appear in \cite{Bandyopadhyay2018LogOP}. RFS
variants include exponential-mixture PHD fusion \cite{Uney2013EMDPHD},
multi-Bernoulli GCI fusion \cite{Wang2017MBGCI}, and robust distributed
labeled RFS fusion \cite{Li2018RobustDistributedLRFS,Gao2023ResilientLMB}.
These works define how received densities should be fused; our question is
what representation needs to be transmitted when the receiver already reduces
labels to moments.

\textbf{Communication-aware fusion.}
Prior work handles limited links through selective transmission, adaptive
scheduling, consensus, and reliability-aware fusion. Examples include
limited-communication labeled RFS fusion \cite{Zhang2016CommConstrainedFusion},
consensus LMB filtering \cite{Shen2022ConsensusLMB}, reliability-weighted
distributed RFS fusion \cite{Zheng2026DistributedWeightedRFS}, geometry-aware
fusion \cite{CaoZhao2025InfoGeometryFusion}, and average-density fusion
approximations \cite{Li2024AADensityFusion}. These approaches often change
message timing, neighbor choice, or fusion weights. Our work separates that
graph-level problem from payload compression: we keep the message paths fixed
and ask whether a lighter label representation is sufficient for the KLA
update.
