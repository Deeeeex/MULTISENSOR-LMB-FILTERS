\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We derived a receiver-induced moment message for a custom presence-conditioned
geometric-average LMB receiver. Sender-side projection preserved every audited
retained post-step field and reduced attempted application-layer payload by
58.28\% across 50 paired trials. The result supports a broader interface-design
principle: derive a message from the receiver's first irreversible map and
certify the downstream numerical contract. Exactness here remains specific to
this receiver and does not extend to mixture-aware or multi-round consumers.
