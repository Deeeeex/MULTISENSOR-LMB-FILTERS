\begin{abstract}
Distributed labeled multi-Bernoulli (LMB) fusion often exchanges full Gaussian-mixture posteriors even when the receiver-side fusion step ultimately consumes label-wise moment-matched Bernoulli statistics. This paper studies a simpler communication lever: keep the effective Kullback-Leibler average (KLA) fusion graph intact, but replace full posterior messages with light posteriors containing per-label existence probabilities and single-Gaussian moments. On a 4+4 multisensor formation benchmark, a periodic light-posterior exchange matches the full-posterior baseline in tracking and consensus metrics over 50 held-out trials while reducing estimated communication by 58.6\%. The result suggests that, for single-round GA/KLA-LMB fusion, payload compression can be safer than event-triggered graph sparsification.
\end{abstract}

\begin{keywords}
Distributed multi-object tracking, labeled random finite sets, LMB filter, KLA fusion, communication-efficient sensing
\end{keywords}
