# Figure Captions

## Figure 1

System overview of the distributed eight-sensor tracking setting, including local measurement update, neighborhood communication, branch-decoupled adaptive GA-LMB fusion, and an FID-FIA cue applied only on the existence-weight path.

## Figure 2

Adaptive fusion-weight factorization, showing the shared quality backbone, decoupled spatial and existence-weight paths, weak structure-aware branch prior, and an FID-FIA cue applied only to the existence branch.

Existence-confidence weighting used in the adaptive rule: (a) the Bernoulli-level decisiveness mapping and bounded score curve, and (b) representative node-level aggregation examples showing how decisive posterior sets produce larger $\bar{c}^{(j)}$ and $q_{\mathrm{exist}}^{(j)}$.
