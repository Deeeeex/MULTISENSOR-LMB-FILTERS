\section{Theoretical Interpretation of Adaptive Branch-Decoupled Fusion}
\label{app:theory}

The proof chain behind the adaptive branch-decoupled rule is deliberately limited. It assumes the same label alignment and moment-compatible Gaussian projection used by the implemented merger, together with standard geometric-average KLA and LMB background \cite{Battistelli2014KLA,Hlinka2014ICI,Uney2013EMDPHD,Bandyopadhyay2018LogOP,Vo2014LRFS,Reuter2014LMB,Vo2019MSGLMB}. It should not be read as a global optimality theory for unconstrained LMB fusion.

\subsection{Entropy-Regularized Interpretation of the Weight Backbone}

Fix a fusion node $s$ at time $k$ with active neighborhood $\mathcal{A}_{k,s}$. The adaptive backbone constructs the positive score
\begin{equation}
s_{0,j}
=
q_{\mathrm{cov},k}^{(j)}
q_{\mathrm{link},k}^{(j)}
q_{\mathrm{exist},k}^{(j)},
\qquad
j \in \mathcal{A}_{k,s},
\end{equation}
where the three terms denote covariance quality, realized link quality, and existence confidence. Define $u_{0,j}=\log s_{0,j}$ and
\begin{equation}
\Delta_{k,s}
=
\left\{
w \in \mathbb{R}^{|\mathcal{A}_{k,s}|} :
w_j \ge 0,\ \sum_j w_j = 1
\right\}.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Proposition C1.}
For any $\tau>0$, the program
\begin{equation}
\max_{w \in \Delta_{k,s}}
\left\{
\sum_j w_j u_{0,j}
+
\tau H(w)
\right\},
\qquad
H(w) = -\sum_j w_j \log w_j,
\label{eq:appendix-backbone}
\end{equation}
has the softmax solution
\begin{equation}
w_j^\star
=
\frac{\exp(u_{0,j}/\tau)}{\sum_u \exp(u_{0,u}/\tau)}
=
\frac{s_{0,j}^{1/\tau}}{\sum_u s_{0,u}^{1/\tau}}.
\end{equation}
Thus $\tau=1$ recovers the implemented normalized product rule. The multiplicative backbone is an entropy-regularized allocation in log-utility space.

\paragraph{Covariance score in the isotropic limit.}
If the local Gaussian components satisfy $T_{k,i}^{(j)}=\sigma_j^2I_d$, then the implemented covariance score
\begin{equation}
q_{\mathrm{cov},k}^{(j)} = \frac{1}{\epsilon + d \sigma_j^2}
\end{equation}
induces the same ordering as smaller $\sigma_j^2$, smaller $\log\det T_{k,i}^{(j)}$, larger $\log\det (T_{k,i}^{(j)})^{-1}$, and larger $\operatorname{tr}((T_{k,i}^{(j)})^{-1})$. The trace-based score therefore agrees with standard information criteria in the isotropic case; near-isotropic differences enter through anisotropy.

\subsection{Algebraic Branch Structure of the Implemented GA-LMB Merger}

The implemented merger uses separate spatial and existence weights because the GA-LMB update already exposes two different algebraic blocks.

\paragraph{Spatial branch.}
For a fixed Bernoulli component $i$, the Gaussian canonical fusion step is
\begin{align}
K_i &= \sum_j w_{x,j} T_{i,j}^{-1}, \\
h_i &= \sum_j w_{x,j} T_{i,j}^{-1}\nu_{i,j},
\end{align}
with $\Sigma_i=K_i^{-1}$ and $\mu_i=\Sigma_i h_i$.

\paragraph{Proposition C2.}
Conditional on $w_x$, $\mathcal{N}(\mu_i,\Sigma_i)$ is the minimizer of
\begin{equation}
\min_{q \in \mathcal{G}}
\sum_j w_{x,j} D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(q \,\|\, \mathcal{N}(\nu_{i,j},T_{i,j})\right),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{G}$ is the Gaussian family. Expanding the Gaussian KL in canonical parameters gives the precision and information-vector averages above, so the spatial update is the reverse-KL Gaussian barycenter.

\paragraph{Existence branch.}
The same merger computes
\begin{equation}
r_i
=
\frac{
\eta_i(w_x)\prod_j r_{i,j}^{w_{r,j}}
}{
\eta_i(w_x)\prod_j r_{i,j}^{w_{r,j}} + \prod_j (1-r_{i,j})^{w_{r,j}}
},
\end{equation}
where $\eta_i(w_x)$ is the spatial-overlap normalization induced by the Gaussian branch.

\paragraph{Proposition C3.}
The fused existence probability satisfies
\begin{equation}
\log \frac{r_i}{1-r_i}
=
\log \eta_i(w_x)
+
\sum_j w_{r,j}\log\frac{r_{i,j}}{1-r_{i,j}}.
\label{eq:appendix-logit-pooling}
\end{equation}
Thus, for fixed spatial weights, the existence branch is a weighted Bernoulli logit pool with an additive spatial-overlap offset.

\subsection{From Bernoulli-RFS Decomposition to an Aligned-LMB Surrogate}

\paragraph{Proposition C4.}
For Bernoulli RFS densities $\beta=(r,p)$ and $\beta_j=(r_j,p_j)$,
\begin{equation}
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\beta \,\|\, \beta_j)
=
d_{\mathrm{Ber}}(r\,\|\,r_j)
+
r\,D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\,\|\,p_j),
\label{eq:appendix-bernoulli-kl}
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
d_{\mathrm{Ber}}(r\,\|\,r_j)
=
(1-r)\log\frac{1-r}{1-r_j}
+
r\log\frac{r}{r_j}.
\end{equation}
The spatial mismatch is therefore naturally weighted by the existence level.

\paragraph{Proposition C5.}
For a fixed Bernoulli component $i$, consider the decoupled surrogate
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{J}_i(r,p;w_x,w_r)
=
\sum_j w_{r,j}\, d_{\mathrm{Ber}}(r\,\|\,r_{i,j})
+
r\sum_j w_{x,j}\, D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\,\|\,p_{i,j}).
\label{eq:appendix-decoupled-bernoulli}
\end{equation}
Its minimizer satisfies
\begin{align}
p_i^\star(x)
&=
\frac{1}{\eta_i(w_x)}
\prod_j p_{i,j}(x)^{w_{x,j}},
\label{eq:appendix-decoupled-spatial}
\\
\log\frac{r_i^\star}{1-r_i^\star}
&=
\log \eta_i(w_x)
+
\sum_j w_{r,j}\log\frac{r_{i,j}}{1-r_{i,j}}.
\label{eq:appendix-decoupled-existence}
\end{align}
The spatial equation follows by normalizing the weighted geometric product. Substituting this optimum gives the $-\log\eta_i(w_x)$ spatial contribution, and differentiating the Bernoulli part gives the logit identity. With aligned labels and product LMB form, the same argument lifts labelwise because the reverse-KL divergence is additive across aligned Bernoulli factors.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
This surrogate does not make the branch-decoupled rule the unique optimizer of a fully unconstrained LMB fusion objective. It only states that the implemented update is compatible with a per-Bernoulli aligned-LMB objective under the merger's label-alignment and projection assumptions.

\subsection{Branch-Decoupled Weight Allocation and Structure-Aware Regularization}

The branch-specific scores are
\begin{align}
s_{x,j}
&=
\left(q_{\mathrm{cov},j}\right)^{\alpha_x}
\left(q_{\mathrm{link},j}\right)^{\beta_x},
\\
s_{r,j}
&=
\left(q_{\mathrm{link},j}\right)^{\beta_r}
\left(q_{\mathrm{exist},j}\right)^{\alpha_r},
\end{align}
with geometric interpolation
\begin{align}
\tilde s_{x,j} &= s_{0,j}^{1-\eta_x}s_{x,j}^{\eta_x}, \\
\tilde s_{r,j} &= s_{0,j}^{1-\eta_r}s_{r,j}^{\eta_r}.
\end{align}
This is convex interpolation in log-utility space. Define $\hat u_{x,j}=\log \tilde s_{x,j}$ and $\hat u_{r,j}=\log \tilde s_{r,j}$ before graph and FID-FIA refinement.

The FID-FIA refinement modifies only the existence branch. Let $a_j$ denote the Fisher-information-distance accumulation from existence-weighted local target pairs, and define
\begin{equation}
q_{\mathrm{fid},j}
=
\rho_{\min} + (1-\rho_{\min})
\left(
\frac{a_j}{\epsilon+\max_{u\in\mathcal{A}_{k,s}} a_u}
\right)^{p_f}.
\end{equation}
For positive $q_{\mathrm{fid},j}$, this contributes $\gamma_f\log q_{\mathrm{fid},j}$ to the existence utility and leaves the spatial utility unchanged. The main zero-floor setting is the boundary case in which a sensor with zero FID-FIA accumulation can receive zero instantaneous existence weight.

The structure-aware refinement then multiplies the spatial score by $\xi_{x,j}^{\gamma_x}$ and the existence score by $\xi_{r,j}^{\gamma_r}$.

\paragraph{Proposition C6.}
Let $p_x$ and $p_r$ be normalized priors satisfying
\begin{align}
p_{x,j} &\propto \xi_{x,j}^{\gamma_x/\tau_x}, \\
p_{r,j} &\propto \xi_{r,j}^{\gamma_r/\tau_r}.
\end{align}
Then the branch-wise allocations are equivalently written as
\begin{align}
w_x^\star
&=
\arg\max_{w \in \Delta_{k,s}}
\left\{
\sum_j w_j \hat u_{x,j}
- \tau_x D_{\mathrm{KL}}(w \,\|\, p_x)
\right\},
\\
w_r^\star
&=
\arg\max_{w \in \Delta_{k,s}}
\left\{
\sum_j w_j \hat u_{r,j}
- \tau_r D_{\mathrm{KL}}(w \,\|\, p_r)
\right\}.
\end{align}
Thus the weak structure-aware term is a KL regularization toward a graph-induced prior on the simplex.

\paragraph{Corollary C6a.}
With the FID-FIA existence refinement, the same program remains valid after replacing $\hat u_{r,j}$ by $\hat u_{r,j}^{\mathrm{fid}}=\hat u_{r,j}+\gamma_f\log q_{\mathrm{fid},j}$. Equivalently,
\begin{equation}
w_{r,j}^{\star}
\propto
\tilde s_{r,j}
\xi_{r,j}^{\gamma_r}
q_{\mathrm{fid},j}^{\gamma_f},
\qquad
w_{x,j}^{\star}
\propto
\tilde s_{x,j}
\xi_{x,j}^{\gamma_x}.
\label{eq:appendix-fid-branch-weights}
\end{equation}
The FID-FIA term is therefore an existence-branch log-utility, not a change to the spatial KLA barycenter.

\paragraph{Proposition C7.}
Define
\begin{align}
U_x(w_x)
&=
\sum_j w_{x,j}\hat u_{x,j} + \tau_x H(w_x), \\
U_r(w_r)
&=
\sum_j w_{r,j}\hat u_{r,j} + \tau_r H(w_r),
\end{align}
with the FID-FIA substitution in $U_r$ when Cardinality-critical mode is active. Under the aligned-label and projection assumptions above, the branch-decoupled adaptive weights solve
\begin{equation}
\max_{w_x,w_r \in \Delta_{k,s}} \left\{ U_x(w_x) + U_r(w_r) \right\}.
\end{equation}
Together with Proposition C5, this gives a block-relaxed interpretation: the state-update block reproduces the aligned Bernoulli/LMB surrogate, and the weight-update block reproduces the branch-wise adaptive allocations.

\paragraph{Scalar-FID-FIA as a constrained special case.}
A single FID-FIA scalar weight for the whole fused posterior imposes $w_x=w_r=w_{\mathrm{fid}}$ and lets the Fisher-separability utility influence both the Gaussian barycenter and the Bernoulli logit pool. The proposed refinement relaxes that constraint: $w_x$ remains governed by the communication-aware spatial utility, while $w_r$ receives the FID-FIA term. This explains why a scalar FID-FIA baseline can improve cardinality while perturbing localization; the branch-decoupled version reserves the information-geometric cue for existence/cardinality fusion.

\subsection{Existence Confidence and Temporal Stabilization}

The existence-confidence term uses $c(r)=|2r-1|$, which is bounded in $[0,1]$ and monotone in the magnitude of the Bernoulli natural parameter:
\begin{equation}
\left|\log\frac{r}{1-r}\right|
=
\log\frac{1+c(r)}{1-c(r)}
=
2\,\mathrm{artanh}(c(r)).
\end{equation}
Thus existence confidence is a bounded proxy for the Bernoulli decisiveness appearing in \eqref{eq:appendix-logit-pooling}.

The temporal stabilization step computes
\begin{equation}
w_k^{\mathrm{ema}} = \alpha w_{k-1} + (1-\alpha)\hat w_k.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Proposition C8.}
For any $\alpha \in [0,1]$, $w_k^{\mathrm{ema}}$ is the minimizer of
\begin{equation}
\min_{w}
\left\{
\alpha \|w-w_{k-1}\|_2^2 + (1-\alpha)\|w-\hat w_k\|_2^2
\right\}.
\end{equation}
The EMA step is therefore a quadratic compromise between the previous weights and the instantaneous weights. The subsequent minimum-weight enforcement remains a pragmatic feasibility projection rather than part of one single exact objective.
