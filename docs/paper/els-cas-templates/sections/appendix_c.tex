\section{Theoretical Interpretation of Adaptive Branch-Decoupled Fusion}
\label{app:theory}

This appendix records the theoretical interpretation of the adaptive branch-decoupled fusion rule used in the main paper. The purpose is narrower than a full new optimality theory. We focus on the exact statements that are supported by the proposed method and the standard KLA/LMB background \citep{Battistelli2014KLA,Hlinka2014ICI,Uney2013EMDPHD,Bandyopadhyay2018LogOP,Vo2014LRFS,Reuter2014LMB,Vo2019MSGLMB}. Throughout this appendix, we assume the same label alignment and moment-compatible Gaussian projection used by the merger.

\subsection{Entropy-Regularized Interpretation of the Weight Backbone}

Fix a fusion node $s$ at time $k$ with active neighborhood $\mathcal{A}_{k,s}$. The adaptive backbone constructs the positive score
\begin{equation}
s_{0,j}
=
q_{\mathrm{cov},k}^{(j)}
q_{\mathrm{link},k}^{(j)}
q_{\mathrm{exist},k}^{(j)},
\qquad
j \in \mathcal{A}_{k,s},
\end{equation}
where $q_{\mathrm{cov}}$, $q_{\mathrm{link}}$, and $q_{\mathrm{exist}}$ denote the covariance-quality, realized link-quality, and existence-confidence terms, respectively. Define
\begin{equation}
u_{0,j}
=
\log q_{\mathrm{cov},k}^{(j)}
+
\log q_{\mathrm{link},k}^{(j)}
+
\log q_{\mathrm{exist},k}^{(j)}.
\end{equation}

Let
\begin{equation}
\Delta_{k,s}
=
\left\{
w \in \mathbb{R}^{|\mathcal{A}_{k,s}|} :
w_j \ge 0,\ \sum_j w_j = 1
\right\}
\end{equation}
be the active-neighbor simplex.

\paragraph{Proposition C1.}
For any temperature $\tau>0$, the optimization problem
\begin{equation}
\max_{w \in \Delta_{k,s}}
\left\{
\sum_j w_j u_{0,j}
+
\tau H(w)
\right\},
\qquad
H(w) = -\sum_j w_j \log w_j,
\label{eq:appendix-backbone}
\end{equation}
has the unique optimizer
\begin{equation}
w_j^\star
=
\frac{\exp(u_{0,j}/\tau)}{\sum_u \exp(u_{0,u}/\tau)}
=
\frac{s_{0,j}^{1/\tau}}{\sum_u s_{0,u}^{1/\tau}}.
\end{equation}
In particular, when $\tau=1$, the implemented normalized product rule is recovered:
\begin{equation}
w_j^\star = \frac{s_{0,j}}{\sum_u s_{0,u}}.
\end{equation}

Thus the multiplicative backbone is exactly an entropy-regularized simplex allocation in log-utility space, not merely an empirical product of factors.

\paragraph{Isotropic interpretation of the covariance term.}
If the local Gaussian components satisfy
\begin{equation}
T_{k,i}^{(j)} = \sigma_j^2 I_d
\end{equation}
for all labels $i$ at sensor $j$, then the implemented covariance score
\begin{equation}
q_{\mathrm{cov},k}^{(j)} = \frac{1}{\epsilon + d \sigma_j^2}
\end{equation}
induces exactly the same ordering as smaller $\sigma_j^2$, smaller $\log\det T_{k,i}^{(j)}$, larger $\log\det (T_{k,i}^{(j)})^{-1}$, and larger $\operatorname{tr}((T_{k,i}^{(j)})^{-1})$. Hence, in the isotropic limit, the retained trace-based score agrees with standard information criteria. In near-isotropic regimes, the disagreement with log-determinant criteria enters only through higher-order anisotropy terms.

\subsection{Exact Branch Structure of the Implemented GA-LMB Merger}

The implemented merger uses separate spatial and existence weights. This separation is not purely heuristic; it is supported by the algebraic structure of the GA-LMB update.

\paragraph{Spatial branch.}
For a fixed Bernoulli component $i$, the Gaussian canonical fusion step is
\begin{align}
K_i &= \sum_j w_{x,j} T_{i,j}^{-1}, \\
h_i &= \sum_j w_{x,j} T_{i,j}^{-1}\nu_{i,j},
\end{align}
with
\begin{equation}
\Sigma_i = K_i^{-1},
\qquad
\mu_i = \Sigma_i h_i.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Proposition C2.}
Conditional on the spatial weights $w_x$, the Gaussian density $\mathcal{N}(\mu_i,\Sigma_i)$ is the unique minimizer of
\begin{equation}
\min_{q \in \mathcal{G}}
\sum_j w_{x,j} D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(q \,\|\, \mathcal{N}(\nu_{i,j},T_{i,j})\right),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{G}$ is the Gaussian family. Hence the implemented spatial update is the exact Gaussian KLA barycenter.

\paragraph{Existence branch.}
The same merger computes
\begin{equation}
r_i
=
\frac{
\eta_i(w_x)\prod_j r_{i,j}^{w_{r,j}}
}{
\eta_i(w_x)\prod_j r_{i,j}^{w_{r,j}} + \prod_j (1-r_{i,j})^{w_{r,j}}
},
\end{equation}
where $\eta_i(w_x)$ is the spatial-overlap normalization term induced by the Gaussian branch.

\paragraph{Proposition C3.}
The fused existence probability satisfies the exact log-odds identity
\begin{equation}
\log \frac{r_i}{1-r_i}
=
\log \eta_i(w_x)
+
\sum_j w_{r,j}\log\frac{r_{i,j}}{1-r_{i,j}}.
\label{eq:appendix-logit-pooling}
\end{equation}
Therefore, for fixed spatial weights, the existence branch is an exact weighted logit pool with an additive spatial-overlap offset.

\subsection{From Bernoulli-RFS Decomposition to an Aligned-LMB Surrogate}

The key theoretical bridge is that Bernoulli-RFS divergence already separates into existence and spatial terms.

\paragraph{Proposition C4.}
Let $\beta=(r,p)$ and $\beta_j=(r_j,p_j)$ be Bernoulli RFS densities. Then
\begin{equation}
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\beta \,\|\, \beta_j)
=
d_{\mathrm{Ber}}(r\,\|\,r_j)
+
r\,D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\,\|\,p_j),
\label{eq:appendix-bernoulli-kl}
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
d_{\mathrm{Ber}}(r\,\|\,r_j)
=
(1-r)\log\frac{1-r}{1-r_j}
+
r\log\frac{r}{r_j}.
\end{equation}

This decomposition shows that spatial mismatch is naturally weighted by existence level.

\paragraph{Proposition C5.}
For a fixed Bernoulli component $i$, consider the decoupled surrogate
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{J}_i(r,p;w_x,w_r)
=
\sum_j w_{r,j}\, d_{\mathrm{Ber}}(r\,\|\,r_{i,j})
+
r\sum_j w_{x,j}\, D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p\,\|\,p_{i,j}).
\label{eq:appendix-decoupled-bernoulli}
\end{equation}
Its minimizer satisfies
\begin{align}
p_i^\star(x)
&=
\frac{1}{\eta_i(w_x)}
\prod_j p_{i,j}(x)^{w_{x,j}},
\label{eq:appendix-decoupled-spatial}
\\
\log\frac{r_i^\star}{1-r_i^\star}
&=
\log \eta_i(w_x)
+
\sum_j w_{r,j}\log\frac{r_{i,j}}{1-r_{i,j}}.
\label{eq:appendix-decoupled-existence}
\end{align}
Hence the implemented spatial and existence update rules are the exact optimizer of a per-Bernoulli decoupled surrogate objective.

If local posteriors share an aligned label set and are represented in product LMB form, then the reverse-KL divergence is additive across labels. Under this same alignment assumption, the per-Bernoulli surrogate lifts directly to an aligned-LMB surrogate objective that decomposes labelwise.

\paragraph{Scope boundary.}
This appendix does not claim that the branch-decoupled rule is the unique optimizer of a fully unconstrained LMB fusion objective. The exact derivation above still relies on the same two architectural assumptions used by the merger: aligned labels and moment-compatible spatial projection.

\subsection{Branch-Decoupled Weight Allocation and Structure-Aware Regularization}

The branch-specific scores are
\begin{align}
s_{x,j}
&=
\left(q_{\mathrm{cov},j}\right)^{\alpha_x}
\left(q_{\mathrm{link},j}\right)^{\beta_x},
\\
s_{r,j}
&=
\left(q_{\mathrm{link},j}\right)^{\beta_r}
\left(q_{\mathrm{exist},j}\right)^{\alpha_r},
\end{align}
with geometric interpolation
\begin{align}
\tilde s_{x,j} &= s_{0,j}^{1-\eta_x}s_{x,j}^{\eta_x}, \\
\tilde s_{r,j} &= s_{0,j}^{1-\eta_r}s_{r,j}^{\eta_r}.
\end{align}
This is exactly a convex interpolation in log-utility space.
Define $\hat u_{x,j}=\log \tilde s_{x,j}$ and $\hat u_{r,j}=\log \tilde s_{r,j}$ for the branch utilities before graph and FID-FIA refinement.

The FID-FIA existence refinement adds one more nonnegative factor only to the existence branch. Let $a_j$ denote the Fisher-information-distance accumulation score computed from existence-weighted local target pairs, and define the bounded normalized score
\begin{equation}
q_{\mathrm{fid},j}
=
\rho_{\min} + (1-\rho_{\min})
\left(
\frac{a_j}{\epsilon+\max_{u\in\mathcal{A}_{k,s}} a_u}
\right)^{p_f}.
\end{equation}
The refined existence score is
\begin{equation}
\tilde s_{r,j}^{\mathrm{fid}}
=
\tilde s_{r,j}\,q_{\mathrm{fid},j}^{\gamma_f}.
\label{eq:appendix-fid-existence-score}
\end{equation}
In log-utility form, this is simply
\begin{equation}
\hat u_{r,j}^{\mathrm{fid}}
=
\hat u_{r,j}+\gamma_f\log q_{\mathrm{fid},j},
\end{equation}
while the spatial utility $\hat u_{x,j}$ is unchanged.
The log-utility statements below are exact for $\rho_{\min}>0$; the main experiment setting $\rho_{\min}=0$ is the boundary limit that allows the existence branch to place zero instantaneous weight on sensors whose FID-FIA accumulation is zero.

After decoupling, the structure-aware refinement multiplies the spatial score by $\xi_{x,j}^{\gamma_x}$ and the existence score, or the FID-FIA-refined existence score when enabled, by $\xi_{r,j}^{\gamma_r}$. This is equivalent to adding $\gamma_x \log \xi_{x,j}$ and $\gamma_r \log \xi_{r,j}$ to the corresponding branch utilities.

\paragraph{Proposition C6.}
Let $p_x$ and $p_r$ be normalized priors satisfying
\begin{align}
p_{x,j} &\propto \xi_{x,j}^{\gamma_x/\tau_x}, \\
p_{r,j} &\propto \xi_{r,j}^{\gamma_r/\tau_r}.
\end{align}
Then the branch-wise optimization problems are equivalently written as
\begin{align}
w_x^\star
&=
\arg\max_{w \in \Delta_{k,s}}
\left\{
\sum_j w_j \hat u_{x,j}
- \tau_x D_{\mathrm{KL}}(w \,\|\, p_x)
\right\},
\\
w_r^\star
&=
\arg\max_{w \in \Delta_{k,s}}
\left\{
\sum_j w_j \hat u_{r,j}
- \tau_r D_{\mathrm{KL}}(w \,\|\, p_r)
\right\}.
\end{align}
Thus the weak structure-aware term is precisely a KL regularization toward a graph-induced prior on the simplex.

\paragraph{Corollary C6a.}
With the FID-FIA existence refinement, the same branch-wise entropy-regularized program remains valid after replacing the existence utility $\hat u_{r,j}$ by $\hat u_{r,j}^{\mathrm{fid}}$. Equivalently,
\begin{equation}
w_{r,j}^{\star}
\propto
\tilde s_{r,j}
\xi_{r,j}^{\gamma_r}
q_{\mathrm{fid},j}^{\gamma_f},
\qquad
w_{x,j}^{\star}
\propto
\tilde s_{x,j}
\xi_{x,j}^{\gamma_x}.
\label{eq:appendix-fid-branch-weights}
\end{equation}
Thus the FID-FIA term is theoretically just another existence-branch log-utility, not a change to the spatial KLA barycenter.

\paragraph{Proposition C7.}
The statement below applies to the unrefined existence utility $\hat u_{r,j}$ and, after the substitution in Corollary C6a, to the FID-FIA-refined utility $\hat u_{r,j}^{\mathrm{fid}}$.
Define the branch utilities
\begin{align}
U_x(w_x)
&=
\sum_j w_{x,j}\hat u_{x,j} + \tau_x H(w_x), \\
U_r(w_r)
&=
\sum_j w_{r,j}\hat u_{r,j} + \tau_r H(w_r).
\end{align}
Then the branch-decoupled adaptive weights are the exact optimizer of the block-separable surrogate program
\begin{equation}
\max_{w_x,w_r \in \Delta_{k,s}} \left\{ U_x(w_x) + U_r(w_r) \right\}.
\end{equation}
Moreover, together with the aligned-LMB surrogate state-update block above, the merger and the adaptive weight-allocation rule are consistent with a single block-relaxed objective whose two blocks reproduce the state-update and weight-update steps.

\paragraph{Scalar-FID-FIA as a constrained special case.}
Using a single FID-FIA scalar weight vector for the whole fused posterior can be viewed as imposing the additional constraint $w_x=w_r=w_{\mathrm{fid}}$ and choosing both branch utilities to be dominated by $\log q_{\mathrm{fid},j}$. The proposed refinement relaxes this constraint: $w_x$ remains governed by the communication-aware spatial utility, while $w_r$ receives the FID-FIA term. This provides a theoretical explanation for the observed empirical tradeoff. A single FID-FIA weight can improve cardinality by moving the existence logit pool toward sensors with high pairwise Fisher separability, but the same move also perturbs the Gaussian precision barycenter. Branch decoupling keeps the information-geometric signal where it is most useful, in the Bernoulli existence pool, without forcing the spatial barycenter to use the same weights.

\paragraph{Proposition C7a.}
Let $p_x$ and $p_r$ be strictly positive priors on the simplex, and let $\mathcal{Q}_x$ and $\mathcal{Q}_r$ be differentiable latent branch-quality functionals. The entropic proximal linearizations
\begin{align}
w_x^{+}
&=
\arg\max_{w \in \Delta_{k,s}}
\left\{
\left\langle \nabla \mathcal{Q}_x(p_x),\, w-p_x \right\rangle
- \tau_x D_{\mathrm{KL}}(w \,\|\, p_x)
\right\},
\\
w_r^{+}
&=
\arg\max_{w \in \Delta_{k,s}}
\left\{
\left\langle \nabla \mathcal{Q}_r(p_r),\, w-p_r \right\rangle
- \tau_r D_{\mathrm{KL}}(w \,\|\, p_r)
\right\}
\end{align}
have unique optimizers
\begin{align}
w_{x,j}^{+} &\propto p_{x,j}\exp\!\left(\frac{(\nabla \mathcal{Q}_x(p_x))_j}{\tau_x}\right), \\
w_{r,j}^{+} &\propto p_{r,j}\exp\!\left(\frac{(\nabla \mathcal{Q}_r(p_r))_j}{\tau_r}\right).
\end{align}
Therefore, if the graph-induced priors satisfy $p_{x,j} \propto \xi_{x,j}^{\gamma_x/\tau_x}$ and $p_{r,j} \propto \xi_{r,j}^{\gamma_r/\tau_r}$, and if the local gradients are summarized by the branch utilities, then the proposed structure-aware adaptive weights coincide exactly with one entropic mirror-ascent / exponentiated-gradient step on those latent branch-quality functionals \citep{KivinenWarmuth1997,BeckTeboulle2003}. This is a first-order relaxation statement, not a claim of a full global optimum for unconstrained LMB fusion.

\paragraph{A reduced-objective sensitivity view.}
Let
\begin{equation}
\Phi(w_x,w_r)
:=
\min_{\{\beta_i\}}
\mathcal{F}(w_x,w_r;\{\beta_i\})
\end{equation}
denote the aligned-LMB surrogate value function from Proposition C5. Under the same local differentiability assumptions used in standard envelope arguments \citep{MilgromSegal2002}, its branch sensitivities are
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial \Phi}{\partial w_{x,j}}
&=
\sum_i r_i^\star
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(p_i^\star\,\|\,p_{i,j}),
\\
\frac{\partial \Phi}{\partial w_{r,j}}
&=
\sum_i d_{\mathrm{Ber}}(r_i^\star\,\|\,r_{i,j}).
\end{align}
Hence the exact reduced aligned-LMB objective penalizes spatial weights through existence-weighted spatial KL mismatch and penalizes existence weights through Bernoulli mismatch. This provides a concrete target for the retained utilities: covariance quality, realized link quality, and existence confidence should be read as stable surrogates for these exact reduced gradients, not as unrelated heuristic factors.

\paragraph{FID-FIA as a Bernoulli-branch separability surrogate.}
The reduced existence sensitivity above is a Bernoulli mismatch term. In labeled multi-target tracking, a large part of this mismatch is driven by whether the measurements provide enough information to distinguish target-generated returns from neighboring targets, clutter, or missed detections. The FID-FIA score measures exactly a local form of target-pair measurement separability through the Fisher metric induced by the sensor model. It is therefore a coherent surrogate for the reliability of the Bernoulli existence decision, even though it is not itself an exact Bernoulli KL gradient. This is the precise theoretical role used here: FID-FIA supplies an information-geometric separability utility for the existence branch, while covariance and link quality continue to supply the spatial and communication utilities.

\subsection{Existence Confidence and Temporal Stabilization}

The existence-confidence term uses
\begin{equation}
c(r) = |2r-1|,
\end{equation}
which is bounded in $[0,1]$. This quantity is a bounded monotone surrogate of Bernoulli decisiveness because
\begin{equation}
\left|\log\frac{r}{1-r}\right|
=
\log\frac{1+c(r)}{1-c(r)}
=
2\,\mathrm{artanh}(c(r)).
\end{equation}
Therefore the existence-confidence score is a bounded monotone proxy for the magnitude of the Bernoulli natural parameter that appears in the exact existence branch \eqref{eq:appendix-logit-pooling}.

The temporal stabilization step computes
\begin{equation}
w_k^{\mathrm{ema}} = \alpha w_{k-1} + (1-\alpha)\hat w_k.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Proposition C8.}
For any $\alpha \in [0,1]$, the EMA point $w_k^{\mathrm{ema}}$ is the unique minimizer of
\begin{equation}
\min_{w}
\left\{
\alpha \|w-w_{k-1}\|_2^2 + (1-\alpha)\|w-\hat w_k\|_2^2
\right\}.
\end{equation}
Thus the EMA step is an exact quadratic compromise between the previous weights and the instantaneous optimizer. The subsequent minimum-weight enforcement should still be interpreted as a pragmatic feasibility projection rather than as part of one single exact optimizer.

\subsection{What This Appendix Establishes}

Under the same aligned-label and moment-compatible assumptions used by the merger, this appendix establishes:
\begin{itemize}
\item an exact entropy-regularized interpretation of the adaptive backbone,
 \item an isotropic-limit agreement between the retained covariance score and standard information criteria,
\item an exact Gaussian KLA barycenter for the spatial branch,
\item an exact logit-pooling identity for the existence branch,
\item an exact Bernoulli-RFS decomposition that motivates branch separation,
\item an aligned-LMB surrogate interpretation of the componentwise decoupled merger,
\item a KL-prior interpretation of the weak structure-aware refinement,
\item an interpretation of FID-FIA as an existence-branch information-geometric log-utility rather than a whole-posterior scalar weight,
\item a first-order entropic mirror-ascent interpretation of the structure-aware weight update,
 \item a concrete reduced-objective sensitivity view in which the exact branch gradients are spatial KL mismatch and Bernoulli mismatch terms,
\item a rationale for reserving FID-FIA for the Bernoulli branch because it proxies target-pair separability relevant to existence decisions,
\item a quadratic-compromise interpretation of the EMA stabilization step.
\end{itemize}
What remains outside the scope of this appendix is a full theorem for completely unconstrained LMB fusion without the aligned-label and moment-projection assumptions used by the proposed merger.
