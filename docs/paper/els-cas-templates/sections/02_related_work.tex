\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

The most relevant prior work falls into three groups: labeled-RFS filtering, conservative distributed density fusion, and adaptive or weighted RFS/LMB fusion. The first group provides the statistical state representation. Mahler's RFS/FISST formulation and probability hypothesis density (PHD) recursion establish the set-valued Bayesian foundation \cite{Mahler2003PHD,Mahler2007FISSTBook,VoMa2006GMPHD}. GLMB and LMB filters add labeled multi-object posteriors with tractable representations of track identity \cite{Vo2014LRFS,Reuter2014LMB,Vo2019MSGLMB,Mahler2019ExactBayes,Vo2024OverviewLRFS}. This paper fuses LMB posteriors.

The second group concerns conservative fusion when cross-correlations are unknown. Covariance intersection, Kullback--Leibler averaging, geometric-average fusion, and inverse-covariance-intersection variants avoid overconfident fusion under unknown dependence \cite{JulierUhlmann1997CI,Mahler2000RobustFusion,Battistelli2014KLA,Hlinka2014ICI,Uhlmann2003CovarianceConsistency,Noack2017ICI,Zhang2020OptimalFusion}. Logarithmic opinion pools and exponential mixtures connect this principle to distributed Bayesian fusion and consensus \cite{Uney2013EMDPHD,Bandyopadhyay2018LogOP}. GCI/KLA fusion has also been extended to multi-Bernoulli and labeled-RFS densities, including label mismatch and peer-to-peer resilience \cite{Wang2017MBGCI,Li2018RobustDistributedLRFS,Gao2023ResilientLMB}. Gao et al. use each receiving node's local LMB density as a trusted reference \cite{Gao2023ResilientLMB}. Their method tests label-associated Bernoulli components from neighbors and reduces confidence as discrepancy from the local reference increases. This attack-resilient objective differs from communication-quality weighting, but its local-trust mechanism provides a relevant comparison.

The third group is most directly related to adaptive weighting. Centralized multiple-view LMB fusion shows that information-aware weights can help when sensors have limited or different fields of view \cite{Wang2018CentralizedLMBFusion,Gostar2021CentralizedCooperativeLMB}. Klupacs et al. fuse label-associated Bernoulli densities and tune each sensor's contribution by object detectability \cite{Klupacs2023ComplementaryFusion}. Distributed LMB tracking has also used automatic track-level weight evolution across different fields of view \cite{Shen2022ConsensusLMB}. Zheng et al. move closer to our setting by factorizing each local RFS density into subdensities \cite{Zheng2026DistributedWeightedRFS}. Local coefficients are assigned before GA or arithmetic-average (AA) fusion under consensus or flooding. Cao and Zhao provide the closest information-geometric GA/KLA precedent \cite{CaoZhao2025InfoGeometryFusion}. Their heterogeneous labeled-RFS method uses Fisher information distance and Fisher information accumulation (FID-FIA) to assign scalar fusion weights. AA density fusion and related minimum-information-loss formulations emphasize different objectives from GA fusion \cite{Li2024AADensityFusion,LiCorchadoSun2017GCIAlternative,Gao2020MIL,Uney2019FiniteSetConsistency}. Recent multirate work also uses Fisher-information-proportional weights after time alignment in both AA and GA fusion \cite{Li2026FIMultirateAADensityFusion}.

Communication constraints provide the deployment motivation for the weighting problem. Bandwidth limits, delays, asynchronous updates, and packet losses are central design factors in distributed fusion over sensor networks \cite{Zhang2016CommConstrainedFusion}. The present work focuses on the packet-delivery side of this problem by feeding realized communication quality directly into the fusion weights.

Against this background, this paper focuses specifically on online weight allocation within peer-to-peer GA/KLA-based LMB fusion. Relative to fixed-weight GA/KLA fusion, the weights react to posterior concentration and realized packet delivery. Relative to adaptive scalar-weight formulations, the rule separates the spatial and existence branches of the LMB posterior and confines the optional FID-FIA cue to the existence path.
