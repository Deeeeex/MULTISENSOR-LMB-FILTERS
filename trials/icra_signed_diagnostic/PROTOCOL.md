# Fixed-input diagnostic of negative current innovations

This diagnostic follows the complete selective experiment. Its primary beat
marked No-age/ER in all four descriptive interval comparisons but failed the
predeclared continuation gate against intermittent M-ECR-S. The signed
ablation was better on reliable links. Neither candidate is promoted or
expanded. All source trajectories are already seen development evidence.

Use every one of the nine development sequences, both radio conditions, and
both the selective and mark-signed trajectories as fixed-input backends.
Preserve all recorded spatial densities and inherited histories. Re-extract
and score five complete scalar rules, including their non-fusion frames:

- retain: the recorded scalar output.
- positive: conservative inherited pooling plus g times positive delta.
- mark_signed: the same base plus g times signed delta.
- negative_all: positive rule plus all current negative delta residuals.
- negative_miss: positive rule plus current negative delta weighted by
  (1-detectionAssociationMass) * pD/(2-pD).

All increments have the residual coefficient (1-beta), with beta as in the
selective round. Additional increments require both eligible sources to
represent the label. Current opportunity, source IDs and original labels are
joined from the existing local update records. The model's actual nominal pD
is 0.9 whenever a predicted track has current direct opportunity and zero
outside its field of view. pD/(2-pD) equals the bounded negative Bayes-factor
contrast for the missed-detection likelihood 1-pD; it is not fitted here.

The missed branch is 1 minus the detection association mass, including the
empty-measurement case where that mass is zero. This tests a source of
negative evidence distinct from the positive mark gate in the preceding
signed ablation. It does not assume that nominal visibility captures real
occlusion. Report any increase in missed-target cost and all sequence values.

No tracker is rerun, no parameter or threshold is optimized, and no sequence
is selected. All five rules are reported on both input histories. These are
fixed-input diagnostics, not causal closed-loop performance or fresh testing.
The full signed evidence construction would require its own preflight,
registration and complete development experiment before any expansion.
