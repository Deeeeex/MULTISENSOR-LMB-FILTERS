# Outcome and wording notes (after frozen validation)

The frozen protocols are not edited after outcomes. Their references to
failed development candidates concern the v2/v3 combinations and their
balanced gate. To avoid overgeneralizing that sentence: v1 `recent`
(Age-all) passed its own initial direction screen versus KLA/FoV, but lost
to the lineage baseline and worsened common-target localization. No v2/v3
combination passed the later full gate. This clarification changes neither
any criterion nor any result, and is reflected precisely in the paper.

Twenty new seeds per family (540 arm runs) show ER's paired OSPA reduction
versus Lineage, with essentially unchanged common-support localization.
The development departure-false-cost benefit does not reproduce: validation
means are 12.255 versus 10.785 m^2 (paired difference +1.47 m^2, descriptive
95% bootstrap interval [-0.705,3.795]). No-new false cost is 0.570 m^2
versus FoV's 0.00375 m^2. These are material limitations, not hidden failures.

The separate Python assignment implementation audited all 518,400 validation
node-frames and all 1,169 frozen source hashes. This checks metrics and
recorded sources, not independent experimental replication or author approval.
