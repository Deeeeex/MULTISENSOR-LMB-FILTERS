# Same-frame reimport after local deletion

The complete census has finished and its independent set reconstruction
passed. All six methods/radio trajectories contain 2,909 identical birth
priors, all surviving their first local update. Population totals show many
inherited local deletions and remote-only reintroductions. This extension
joins the actual events; totals alone do not establish that they are the
same labels.

On all six frozen nominal trajectories and all 240 frames, enumerate every
retained post-fusion label without a local source. Separate labels absent
from this frame's local prediction from labels present in prediction and
deleted by the current local update. For the latter, preserve the exact
local predicted/posterior existence, current opportunity and nominal pD,
remote source identity/existence, recorded missing-label existence bound,
source participation, fusion weights, actual returned existence, and
whether the returned spatial mean is within the existing GT 5 / 2 m gate.

Check every returned label against the stored native local, fusion-source
and fusion-output records. Count consecutive frames of same-receiver,
same-label delete-and-reimport, with interrupted runs reset. Report full
trajectories and the original frames 53--122; keep both methods with and
without improvement. The no-reception frames cannot create an event.

At a qualified missing-label input, compare the actual deleted marginal
with the recorded 0.001 upper bound without changing the fusion output.
Report how often the local value is strictly smaller and its magnitude.
No alternate scores, new native trajectory, pruning threshold or fusion
rule is produced in this extension. A future refinement must be separately
frozen and compared against equally informed simple baselines. Counts and
repeated returns alone do not prove the cause of the complete tracking loss.
