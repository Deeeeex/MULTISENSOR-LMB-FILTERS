# Priority after the fixed-input screen

The complete screen and independent verification passed. The preregistered
primary met all eight fixed-input comparisons, with OSPA reductions of
0.04–0.06% across the four dataset/link groups. This permits, but does not
require, a separately registered recursive experiment. No recursive result
or communication benefit has been established for this rule.

On 2026-09-10 the user explicitly asked to move to new directions promptly
when the current method has weak effects. Full recursion for this small
direction-constraint change is therefore deferred. The screen is retained
as a positive but marginal fixed-input result, not relabeled as a failed
gate or evidence that the complete method improves tracking.

The next experiment investigates relative spatial misalignment using current
raw sensor observations, with identical corrections for GCE and No-age.
This is a different source-model hypothesis from tuning additional evidence
strength, history penalties, or label admission. Its protocol and results
belong to a separate study.
